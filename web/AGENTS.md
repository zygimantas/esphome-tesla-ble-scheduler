# web/

- The page is built into the firmware (`js_include` and `css_include` in device.yaml), so a change needs a build and a flash. The board serves it as /0.js and /0.css.
- It shows and picks times in the phone's time zone, assuming the board's is the same.
- render() runs from requestAnimationFrame, which hidden and background browser tabs don't fire. When you test in a headless or hidden browser, replace requestAnimationFrame with a direct call, or the page stays empty.
- To try it without the board, serve a page that loads web_ui.css and web_ui.js as /0.css and /0.js, a fake /events (Server-Sent Events with `state` events like `{"id":"text_sensor/Charging status","value":"Charging","state":"Charging"}`) and a handler that accepts the POSTs; one that never answers them tries the 8 s timeout.
- Biome formats and lints this folder; it replaced Prettier, whose CSS output differs slightly. A biome-ignore comment goes right above the declaration it's about, not above its rule. `[hidden] { display: none !important; }` stays: without it, elements with their own display rule ignore the hidden attribute.
- The layout is the owner's. Ready by is one dropdown of half-hours, as separate day and time dropdowns confused them. Only Create key under Board, Reset savings, Restart and Factory reset ask first: the setup's Create key doesn't, as its step says what to do. A new board's page is only its setup, in steps of their own cards, titled without numbers: VIN, with why the board needs it and where to find it above the field, Key, with why and what kind of key (charging only) and what to do in numbered steps, and Prices, with the bill's two parts above its fields, each under its name: Country (its name, with the market area where it has several), Supplier's price (Dynamic (spot, exchange), the EU's word for a price that follows the market as often as it settles, or Fixed (or a monthly average), as the contract says, where fixed leaves out `market:`, writes the country's currency where it isn't the euro, and needs cheaper hours) and Cheaper hours, named so as Time of day told the owner nothing: None, Based on grid plan (where the country has plans, which then show in a list of their own) or My own (From - until, one stretch a day, weekends like weekdays or cheaper all day, and the two prices, called a price with a fixed price and a grid fee with the exchange's, which the form reads back only in the shape it writes them). The plan list holds only plans, by request. No Choose where the page can guess, by request: a new board starts at Dynamic and, where the country has plans, Based on grid plan, with the plan chosen where there's only one; Choose stays only where nothing can be guessed: several plans, a country with several market areas or an unknown one, an unknown time zone. No lists of suppliers or grid operators: a supplier's name doesn't say which kind of price it is, and the plans name their operator. VIN comes with Validate VIN, whose field takes only what a VIN can hold as it's typed (capitals and digits, I, O and Q as 1 and 0, at most 17), and which checks the VIN's form before saving it and says what's wrong on the card (17 letters and digits without I, O or Q, a Tesla maker code and the check digit, which Tesla's US, Shanghai and Berlin VINs all carry; a new factory's code goes in TESLA_MAKERS), as the settings form's Save does, Prices with Save and Back, Key with only Create key, and no Cancel. A done step folds to its title and what it holds, and opens again with a click, as do the steps up to the furthest one reached; later ones show only their titles. Last comes Advanced, always there and folded, with Upload settings, Restart board and Factory reset. VIN's Save saves it with the guesses and no prices, which the board takes at once, so it can find the car; Key moves on by itself once the car answers the key, unless it was opened by hand (its card or Back); the last step saves the rest, and the board restarts. Settings without prices, or a key the car doesn't know, bring the setup back at the step they need. Nothing else is on it: no phone messages or Bluetooth signal, by request, and Upload settings only under Advanced; the rest start from guesses and wait for Change settings. Don't add an expected cost of the schedule ahead (the savings card shows what charging already cost), schedule warnings (they go in the phone message), settings beyond the settings form (the rest are in the settings file, downloaded and uploaded there), price charts or the range in km.
- The look follows ESPHome Web (web.esphome.io), as the owner asked: its colors (#009ac7, and its light and dark backgrounds), white cards with a thin border and 12px corners, titles in sentence case, fields and buttons with 6px corners (primary ones filled, others outlined), values in grey pills like its status chips, and system fonts. Its header bar too, in the same blue in light and dark: the owner's logo, a calendar with a plug in a circle, white with its details in the bar's blue (currentColor) as ESPHome's logo is; the project's name, ESPHome Tesla BLE Scheduler, with the subtitle Charges when it's cheapest; and a pill with the link to the board (Connecting …, Connected, No connection).
- Text: a plain hyphen with spaces in time ranges, a space before an ellipsis ("Getting prices …"), and no en or em dashes.
