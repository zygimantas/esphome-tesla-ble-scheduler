# plans/cz/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges.

- **Dynamic contracts:** Available, but rare: most households pay a fixed price or an open-ended price list. A household's contract with a dynamic price takes effect only once its supply point has interval metering (Energy Act 458/2000 Sb., § 11ca(3)), and suppliers with 200,000 supply points or more must offer one to such customers (§ 30(3)(a)). Interval metering means types A and B or the smart meters C1 - C3 (decree 359/2020 Sb., § 5). Most homes still have a C4 meter, read once a year. The decree requires a smart meter above 6 MWh a year, with solar or storage, or in energy sharing, and a home with an EV often passes 6 MWh. ČEZ Distribuce's SmartME project installs about 1.03 million smart meters (https://www.cezdistribuce.cz/cs/dotacni-projekty/cez-distribuce-smartme-inteligentni-mereni), and the national plan aims at 2 million by 2030. No count of households on spot contracts was found. Spot contracts bill OTE's day-ahead price for the Czech area, set per quarter-hour in euros since 1 October 2025, converted at the ČNB's middle rate of the day. ČEZ's and bezDodavatele's price lists bill each quarter-hour; Delta Green's and Yello's still say each hour (OTE also publishes each hour's average of its four quarter-hours). Hourly offers for homes without interval metering, like bezDodavatele's 24/7, spread the month's use by a standard profile (TDD), so when the car charges doesn't change that bill.
- **VAT:** 21%, the basic rate, on the electricity and on every charge below (VAT Act 235/2004 Sb., § 47(1)(a)). The same in October - December 2025.
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:**
  - Electricity tax (daň z elektřiny): 28.30 Kč/MWh, 0.02830 without VAT and 0.03424 with (Act 261/2007 Sb., part 47, § 6(2)).
  - System services (systémové služby): 164.24 Kč/MWh, 0.16424 without VAT and 0.19873 with (ERÚ's cenový výměr 13/2025, point 3.1.1: https://eru.gov.cz/sites/default/files/obsah/prilohy/erv172025.pdf).
  - POZE, the fee for renewables: 0 all of 2026, as the state pays it (cenový výměr 15/2025 of 29 December 2025: https://eru.gov.cz/sites/default/files/obsah/prilohy/erv192025.pdf). ERÚ's later change to the same price measure, in April 2026, concerns only local distribution systems.
  - Their sum with VAT: 0.23297. The suppliers' 2026 price lists show the same tax and system services, and Electree's and Yello's show POZE at 0. Nothing is a share of the bill. Monthly, so left out: the breaker fee, the supplier's fee and the non-network infrastructure fee (12.87 Kč, for OTE, the data centre and ERÚ). OTE's 2.59 Kč per MWh traded (point 7.4) is charged to suppliers, so it's inside their margin.
  - October - December 2025 cost more: system services were 170.92 Kč/MWh, and POZE was 495 Kč/MWh, the cap in Act 165/2012 Sb., § 28a(2), which every home paid, as the price per breaker, 84.70 Kč/A a month for each phase, came out far higher (https://eru.gov.cz/sites/default/files/obsah/prilohy/erv132024.pdf). Their sum with VAT was 0.84001, 0.60703 more than in 2026.
- **Supplier's margin:** about 370 Kč/MWh, 0.44770 per kWh with VAT, the middle of four fixed adders on the market price, each the same in the high and the low tariff:
  - ČEZ Prodej, Elektřina SPOT, from 1 January 2026 and the same since 1 October 2025: 0.39900 without VAT, 0.48279 with, and 128 Kč a month: https://www.cez.cz/webpublic/file/edee/ceniky/moo_ele_cez_spot_01012026_12026.pdf
  - bezDodavatele, SPOT, from 1 January 2026: 0.39000, 0.47190 with VAT (0.35000 with a control system it supports), and 4.18 Kč a day with a two-rate tariff: https://www.bezdodavatele.cz/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/26_01_EL_Cenik_DOM_Spot_info.pdf
  - Electree, HOME SPOT-A, from 1 September 2025: 0.34900, 0.42229 with VAT, and 99 Kč a month; its HOME ELECTREE DRIVE for EV owners, from 16 April 2026: 0.31900, 0.38599 with VAT, and 130 Kč a month: https://electree.cz/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Elektrina-HOME-SPOT-A.pdf and https://electree.cz/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Elektrina-HOME-ELECTREE-DRIVE.pdf
  - Delta Green, Delta SPOT, from 1 June 2026: 0.30000, 0.36300 with VAT, and 159 Kč a month; its SPOT from 1 January 2026: 0.35000, 0.42350 with VAT, and 129 Kč a month: https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/63072b68a235768212bfaa95/6a16ab2e98aeb497cc7b26b9_Delta%20Green%20-%20Cen%C3%ADk%20Delta%20SPOT%20-%20%C4%8CEZ%20-%20Dom%C3%A1cnosti.pdf
  - Yello, PRE's brand, Yello Spot, from 1 January 2026, adds 15% to the market price instead (takes 85% of a negative one), and 199 Kč a month: https://www.yello.cz/files/ceniky/yello-spot-cenik/
- **The plan:** D27d, the rate for EV owners, from ČEZ Distribuce, the largest operator, as a custom plan: `mon-sun: vt 02:00 nt 06:00 vt 20:00 nt`, with `nt: 0.14097` and `vt: 2.72546` (116.50 and 2,252.45 Kč/MWh without VAT). The cheap fee starts at 20:00. ERÚ gives D27d to anyone who shows they own or lease an EV, with a low tariff of at least 8 hours a day between 18:00 and 08:00, set by the operator (cenový výměr 14/2025, D 27d: https://eru.gov.cz/sites/default/files/obsah/prilohy/erv182025.pdf). It pays with an EV: its breaker fee is D02d's (255 against 256 Kč a month at 3x25 A), and 3,300 kWh charged in the low tariff cost about 7,800 Kč a year less than on D02d, while the rest of the home pays 0.21038 more per kWh. For supply points with interval metering, which a dynamic contract needs, ČEZ Distribuce fixes D27d's low tariff at 02:00 - 06:00 and 20:00 - 24:00 every day (variant EMOV1: https://www.cezdistribuce.cz/file/edee/distribuce/cezdistribuce_pasmaplatnostintavt_prubehove_mereni.pdf). With ripple control (HDO), the hours differ by place. In October - December 2025 the rates were nt 0.24926 and vt 2.74320 (https://eru.gov.cz/sites/default/files/obsah/prilohy/erv142024.pdf). On one rate, D02d, it's cz/cez-d02d. All nine plans' prices match cenový výměr 14/2025 times 1.21, to 5 decimals.
- **Prices to download:** SMARD's series 261, Czechia's quarter-hour day-ahead prices in euros per MWh, free and without an account, a week a file, named by its Monday's 00:00 Prague time in ms: https://www.smard.de/app/chart_data/261/DE/261_DE_quarterhour_1759096800000.json (from Monday 29 September 2025), listed in https://www.smard.de/app/chart_data/261/DE/index_quarterhour.json. They are OTE's prices, the ones spot contracts bill: both have 97.21 EUR/MWh for 1 October 2025, 00:00 - 00:15 (OTE's day: https://www.ote-cr.cz/pubweb/attachments/01/2025/month10/day01/DT_15MIN_01_10_2025_CZ.xlsx). The files have every quarter-hour from 1 October 2025 to the morning of 1 October 2026. Suppliers convert at the ČNB's rate, the board at the ECB's.
