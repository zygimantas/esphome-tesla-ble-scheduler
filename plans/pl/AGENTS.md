# plans/pl/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges.

- **Dynamic contracts:** Very few households have one: URE counted 4,836 at the end of 2025, up from 135, which is 0.03% of households, and 73% of them are prosumers with solar panels; the contract needs a remotely read meter, which 48% of household metering points had, 7.99 million of 16.7 million ([URE's report](https://www.ure.gov.pl/download/9/16268/Raport2025.pdf), 29 May 2026), and 41% of households in Tauron Dystrybucja's area ([WysokieNapiecie.pl](https://wysokienapiecie.pl/116612-mamy-juz-prawie-10-mln-inteligentnych-licznikow-energii/), 30 January 2026). Other households paid a price set in advance, the same every hour on G11 and in two zones on G12: 500 zł/MWh without VAT and excise by law from October to December 2025, when the approved tariffs averaged 572.64 zł/MWh ([URE](https://www.ure.gov.pl/pl/urzad/informacje-ogolne/aktualnosci/12880,Prezes-Urzedu-Regulacji-Energetyki-zatwierdzil-zmiany-taryf-na-sprzedaz-energii-.html), 30 September 2025), then the tariffs approved for 2026, 495.16 on average ([URE](https://www.ure.gov.pl/pl/urzad/informacje-ogolne/aktualnosci/13002,Prezes-Urzedu-Regulacji-Energetyki-zatwierdzil-taryfy-na-sprzedaz-i-dystrybucje-.html), 17 December 2025), among them Tauron's G11 at 497.0 and its G12 at 543.0 by day and 413.0 by night ([Tauron's tariff](https://bip.ure.gov.pl/download/3/20340/TauronSprzedaz.pdf)). A dynamic contract gave up that cap and usually cost more: its customers paid 630 zł/MWh on average in 2025, fixed fees included, against 442 for Fixing I, and those of only four of ten sellers paid under 500 ([URE's report](https://www.ure.gov.pl/download/9/16268/Raport2025.pdf)); the market alone averaged 666.68 zł/MWh in September 2026 ([Bankier](https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/Srednia-cena-kontraktu-na-dostawy-energii-na-TGE-w-IX-wzrosla-do-666-68-zl-MWh-9206794.html), 2 October 2026). Tauron, PGE, Enea, Energa and Pstryk offer them, but they bill TGE's Fixing I ([URE's report](https://www.ure.gov.pl/download/9/16268/Raport2025.pdf)), a local auction in złoty, not the European auction's price (TGE's Fixing II), which Nord Pool publishes and the board downloads. They price by the hour, not the quarter-hour: Pstryk, Tauron and Enea list hourly prices ([Pstryk](https://pstryk.pl/cennik), [Tauron](https://www.tauron.pl/dla-domu/prad/prad-z-usluga/ceny-dynamiczne), [Enea](https://www.enea.pl/ceny-dynamiczne)) and Energa applies each hour's Fixing I to its four quarter-hours ([Energa's terms](https://www.energa.pl/dam/jcr:2971d640-ec36-434b-ab84-e310a8b1ea07/Regulamin%20naszej%20oferty%20Oferta%20dynamiczna%20II%20dla%20domu%20obowi%C4%85zuj%C4%85cy%20od%201%20listopada%202025%20roku.pdf)), although TGE has quoted Fixing II by the quarter-hour since 1 October 2025 and Fixing I since 11 June 2026 ([TGE, 1 October 2025](https://tge.pl/komunikaty-tge-czytaj?cmn_id=92000&title=SDAC%3A+Zmiana+publikacji+wynik%C3%B3w+na+stronie+TGE); [TGE, 15 May 2026](https://tge.pl/komunikaty-tge-czytaj?cmn_id=92080&title=Publikacja+wynik%C3%B3w+notowa%C5%84+RDN+na+stronie+TGE+%E2%80%93+wdro%C5%BCenie+15+MTU+na+aukcji+I+fixing+RDN)). For 10:00 - 10:15 on 12 October 2026, Fixing I was 484.01 zł/MWh and Fixing II 550.78 zł/MWh (125.69 EUR), per [TGE's table](https://tge.pl/energia-elektryczna-rdn). PGE moves to Fixing II from 2027 ([PGE's offer](https://www.gkpge.pl/dla-domu/oferta/dynamiczna-energia-z-pge)). Until the board can take Fixing I, its schedules for Poland follow another price than the bill.
- **VAT:** 23%.
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** 0.0485 zł without VAT, 0.05966 zł with it:
  - the quality fee, 0.0332 zł from 1 February 2026 (0.0331 in January), set by PSE's tariff ([Tauron's notice](https://www.tauron-dystrybucja.pl/-/media/offer-documents/dystrybucja/aktualna-taryfa/nowa-stawka-oplaty-jakosciowej-od-1-lutego-2026r.ashx)), billed apart from the network rate;
  - the renewable energy fee (OZE), 0.0073 zł ([URE](https://www.ure.gov.pl/download/9/15811/InformacjaPUREwysokosstawkioplatyOZE271025.pdf)), and the cogeneration fee, 0.0030 zł (Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1664);
  - excise, 0.0050 zł (Excise Act, art. 89(3)), which some suppliers put inside their adder.

  The capacity fee is monthly for households, by yearly use, and the transition fee ended with 2025.
- **Supplier's margin:** Tauron's own dynamic offer, 0.0842 zł without VAT and excise, 0.10357 with VAT; the big suppliers' range from 0.080 to 0.0855 zł without VAT, with monthly fees of 0 to 27.06 zł ([URE's offer list](https://maszwybor.ure.gov.pl/), 2 October 2026).
- **The plan:** `pl/tauron-g12`: Tauron Dystrybucja is the largest operator. G12's night, 22:00 - 06:00 and 13:00 - 15:00, follows winter time, so it starts at 23:00 on the clock in summer: an owner would set the car's schedule at 23:00. Its prices held from January to September 2026.
- **Prices to download:** SMARD's series 257, in euros, like https://www.smard.de/app/chart_data/257/DE/257_DE_quarterhour_1766962800000.json. It matched Nord Pool's PL prices in euros on all 4,992 quarter-hours from 10 August to 30 September 2026, and at the ECB's daily rate it's within half a percent of Nord Pool's in złoty. Both are Fixing II, not what dynamic contracts bill.
- **Uncertain:** whether Tauron's adder includes excise (inferred, not stated), and the suppliers' adders before mid-2026. The number of dynamic contracts after 31 December 2025, and what their customers paid in 2026, stay unpublished until URE's next report, due by 30 May 2027; the only later figure is a guess that Pstryk alone has more than ten thousand customers ([Green-news.pl](https://www.green-news.pl/4781-pstryk-dynamiczne-ceny-axpo-Eon-RWE-axpo), 30 July 2026). PGE's move to Fixing II rests on one sentence on its offer page, which refuses automated reads, so it was seen only in search excerpts that also say the offer is on sale to 30 June 2026, and no such change was found at another seller. PGE's terms, which would say whether it prices by the hour or the quarter-hour, couldn't be read either, and Enea's page still says hourly after TGE began quoting Fixing I by the quarter-hour.
