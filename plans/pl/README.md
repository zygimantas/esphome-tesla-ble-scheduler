# Polish plans

The household plans G11, G12 and G12w of Poland's five big distribution operators, with prices with VAT, in złoty. Choose yours under **Grid plan** on the board's page.

| Operator | G11 | G12 | G12w |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PGE Dystrybucja | `pl/pge-g11` | `pl/pge-g12` | `pl/pge-g12w` |
| Tauron Dystrybucja | `pl/tauron-g11` | `pl/tauron-g12` | `pl/tauron-g12w` |
| Enea Operator | `pl/enea-g11` | | `pl/enea-g12w` |
| Energa-Operator | `pl/energa-g11` | `pl/energa-g12` | `pl/energa-g12w` |
| Stoen Operator | `pl/stoen-g11` | `pl/stoen-g12` | `pl/stoen-g12w` |

- **G11:** the same fee every hour.
- **G12:** cheap from 22:00 to 06:00 and from 13:00 to 15:00, every day. With PGE, the afternoon's cheap hours are from 15:00 to 17:00 from April to September.
- **G12w:** cheap all day at weekends and on public holidays, and on workdays at night: from 22:00 to 06:00 and 13:00 to 15:00 as G12 with PGE, Tauron and Energa, from 21:00 to 06:00 with Enea, and from 22:00 to 06:00 with Stoen.
- **Enea's G12** isn't here: Enea sets its hours for each meter. Write a custom plan with its prices, as [Custom plan](../README.md#custom-plan) says.

The hours are on winter time all year, as the operators set meter clocks, so in summer they come an hour later on the clock: the night from 23:00. If your meter follows summer time, download your plan from this folder, change its `clock: winter` to `clock: local` and upload it as a custom plan, as [Custom plan](../README.md#custom-plan) says.

The plans leave out the monthly fees and the charges that are the same every hour: the quality fee, the renewable energy fee (OZE) and the cogeneration fee.

[Custom plan](../README.md#custom-plan) says how to change the plans' prices for yourself: download a plan's file from this folder, change it and upload it with **Upload custom plan**.
