# plans/be/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges.

- **Dynamic contracts:** offered widely, taken rarely. On 30 September 2025 households had 20,552 of them, 0.40% of about 5.2 million contracts: 20,453 in Flanders (0.70%), 93 in Brussels and 6 in Wallonia ([CREG RA3159](https://www.creg.be/sites/default/files/assets/Publications/Reports/RA3159NL.pdf), 15 January 2026). Three households in four have a variable price, indexed each month or quarter on the market (46% of all on spot indexes), and one in four a fixed price ([CREG F3188](https://www.creg.be/sites/default/files/assets/Publications/Studies/F3188NL.pdf), 12 March 2026). At the end of 2025, 16 suppliers offered one, among them the five with over 200,000 customers, which must: Engie, Luminus, Eneco, TotalEnergies and Mega. They need a digital meter that reports quarter-hours (SMR3), and bill Belgium's day-ahead price, one price in EPEX SPOT's and Nord Pool's coupled auction: by quarter-hour at Engie (for contracts from 1 October 2025) and Bolt, by hour (Belpex H) at Luminus, TotalEnergies, Eneco and Mega, as their October 2026 price sheets say.
- **VAT:** 6% for households all through October 2025 - September 2026, on energy, network fees and federal excise alike, permanent since 1 April 2023 (law of 19 March 2023 reforming the taxes on the energy bill); 21% for business use.
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** in Fluvius Imewo, without and with VAT:
  - public service obligations, 0.02492 and 0.02641 EUR, and surcharges, 0.00144 and 0.00152 EUR, in 2026 ([Fluvius Imewo's 2026 sheet](https://www.fluvius.be/sites/fluvius/files/2025-11/distributienettarieven-elektriciteit-afname-fluvius-imewo-01012026-31122026.xlsx), whose kWh-tarief is the plan; transmission is included);
  - the federal excise: until 31 July 2026, as in October - December 2025, the special excise, 0.04748 EUR, and the energy contribution, 0.00193 EUR, together 0.04941 and 0.05237 EUR; from 1 August 2026 the special excise alone, 0.04600 and 0.04876 EUR, as the programme law of 30 May 2026 cut it, merged the energy contribution into it and dropped the brackets by consumption ([customs circular 2026/C/82](https://blog.forumforthefuture.be/nl/article/circulaire-2026c82-betreffende-energieproducten-en-elektriciteit/32514); Fluvius' sheets for [July](https://www.fluvius.be/sites/fluvius/files/2026-06/tarieven-fraude-en-zegelbreuk-elektriciteit-fluvius-antwerpen-01072026-30092026.pdf) and [August](https://www.fluvius.be/sites/fluvius/files/2026-07/tarieven-fraude-en-zegelbreuk-elektriciteit-fluvius-imewo-01082026-30092026.pdf); 0.04876 EUR with VAT on Engie's, Luminus', Eneco's, Mega's and Bolt's October 2026 sheets);
  - green power and CHP certificates, which every supplier passes on per kWh: 0.01472 and 0.01560 EUR in 2026, 0.01590 EUR with VAT in October - December 2025 ([Luminus' table](https://www.luminus.be/nl/prive/hulp-en-contact/hoe-worden-de-tarieven-voor-groene-stroom-en-wkk-bepaald-nl/); 0.01510 at Eneco to 0.01582 at Engie in October 2026).

  With VAT that's 0.09591 EUR until July 2026 and 0.09230 EUR from August: 0.09530 EUR over the 365 nights, 304 and 61. October - December 2025 had Fluvius' 2025 obligations and surcharges, 0.03635 EUR with VAT, so 0.10462 EUR. No charge is a share of the bill but VAT.
- **Left out:** the Flemish Energiefonds levy, 0 for a home and 10.07 EUR a month without VAT for a second home or an extra meter in a garage ([Vlaanderen](https://www.vlaanderen.be/belastingen-en-begroting/vlaamse-belastingen/energieheffingen/bijdrage-energiefonds-heffing-op-afnamepunten-van-elektriciteit/tarief-van-de-bijdrage-energiefonds)); data management, 18.92 EUR a year; suppliers' fixed fees.
- **Capacity tariff:** Fluvius also bills the average of each month's highest quarter-hour, at least 2.5 kW: in Imewo 54.20098 EUR per kW a year without VAT, 57.45304 with it, in 2026 (53.24224 without VAT in 2025). The board doesn't count it ([#82](https://github.com/zygimantas/esphome-tesla-ble-scheduler/issues/82)). Charging at 11 kW can add several hundred euros a year, whenever the car charges, unless it charges slower.
- **Supplier's margin:** 0.01676 EUR with VAT (0.01581 without): the CREG's average over all dynamic products in 2025 (RA3159), a mark-up of 0.01273 EUR plus a coefficient of 1.0358 on the market price, whose 3.58% is taken at 86.08 EUR per MWh, about what the board's quarter-hours cost (the four cheapest from 18:00 to 07:00, every night of the year). Followed exactly, it's 9.795% on the market price with VAT (1.06 x 1.0358) and 0.01349 EUR. Six offers for Flanders in October 2026, adder without and with VAT, coefficient, fixed fee with VAT:
  - [Engie Dynamic](https://www.engie.be/api/engie/be/ms/pricing/v1/public/pricesAndConditionsPDF?document=E_DYNAMIC_R_GREY_C_I_12_V_N&monthOffset=0&segment=R&language=N): 0.00713 and 0.00756 EUR, 1.039 on the quarter-hour price, 100.70 EUR a year;
  - [Luminus Dynamic Online](https://www.luminus.be/api-next/get-pricelist?documentSlug=dynamic&energyType=electricity&language=nl&tabValue=Flanders): 0.01959 and 0.02077 EUR, 1.019 on the hourly price, 75 EUR;
  - [TotalEnergies myDynamic](https://totalenergies.be/static/marketing-documents/b2c/tariff-card/latest/MYDYNAMIC_ELECTRICITY_VL_NL.pdf): 0.03410 and 0.03615 EUR, which include the certificates' 0.01570 EUR with VAT, so 0.01929 and 0.02045 EUR without them, 1.031 hourly, 90 EUR;
  - [Eneco Zon & Wind Dynamisch](https://cdn.eneco.be/downloads/nl/general/tk/BC_032_012610_NL_ENECO_POWER_DYNAMIC.pdf): 0.00485 and 0.00514 EUR, 1.02 hourly, 15 EUR;
  - [Mega Dynamic](https://my.mega.be/resources/tarif/Mega-NL-EL-B2C-VL-102026-Dynamic0110.pdf): 0.00600 and 0.00636 EUR, 1.05 hourly, 85 EUR, waived the first year;
  - [Bolt Variabel Online](https://files.boltenergie.be/pricelists/var/online_res_el_nl_14.pdf) with its dynamic option: 0.01690 and 0.01791 EUR, 1.168 on the quarter-hour price, 5.99 EUR a month.

  At 86.08 EUR per MWh they come to 0.00697 - 0.03324 EUR with VAT, Engie's 0.01112, with a median of 0.01681. The CREG's average fixed fee was 60 EUR a year.
- **The plan:** `be/fluvius-imewo`. Imewo (Ghent, Bruges, Ostend) is Fluvius' largest area, with 532,287 of the 2,676,055 low-voltage offtake points in September 2026 (19.9%), ahead of Antwerpen (18.4%) and West (15.2%) ([Fluvius' open data](https://opendata.fluvius.be/), datasets 1_17 and 1_23). Its fee, 0.02749 EUR with VAT, is the same every hour: there's no cheap fee at night. A day/night meter's night rate starts at 22:00 on weekdays and lasts the weekend, but only fixed and variable contracts price it lower. All eight Fluvius plans match the 2026 sheets in their first line (the kWh-tarief for a digital meter, with 6%), valid all year, and October 2026's supplier sheets have the same totals; Imewo's fee was 0.02609 EUR in 2025. Engie's Empower Flextime, a variable contract with about 25,000 customers in under a year, prices energy lowest from 01:00 to 07:00 every day ([Engie](https://www.engie.be/nl/empower-flextime/)).
- **Wallonia:** ORES Bihoraire is off-peak 22:00 - 07:00 and 11:00 - 17:00 every day, so its cheap fee starts at 22:00 (Impact's ECO at 01:00), at 0.07390 against 0.13270 EUR at peak for the whole network fee, plus 0.02740 EUR transport and 0.03030 EUR certificates, with VAT; but only 6 households there had a dynamic contract.
- **Prices to download:** SMARD's series 4996 (Belgium), a week a file from Monday 00:00 German time, in EUR per MWh, like https://www.smard.de/app/chart_data/4996/DE/4996_DE_quarterhour_1766962800000.json (29 December 2025 - 4 January 2026), with the weeks in https://www.smard.de/app/chart_data/4996/DE/index_quarterhour.json. It has all 35,040 quarter-hours from 1 October 2025 to 30 September 2026 and matched Nord Pool's BE prices, which the board downloads, on all 96 quarter-hours of 15 September 2026; Nord Pool wants an account for older days (401 for 15 November 2025). It's the price dynamic contracts bill; the hourly ones bill the hour's value.
- **Uncertain:** the law's own rates, as the government's and the Chamber's sites refused automated requests: 46.00 EUR per MWh from August is Fluvius' figure without VAT and five suppliers' with it (TotalEnergies' sheet still lists the old 0.20-cent energy contribution), and 47.48 EUR before is Fluvius' figure to three decimals. Whether Belpex H is the mean of the hour's four quarter-hours since October 2025; with those contracts, quarter-hours within an hour cost the same. Certificates differ by supplier and change by quarter. No count of dynamic contracts after September 2025; the VREG's market monitor 2025 (0.7% of Flemish households in June 2025) now answers 404, but agrees. The area count includes small businesses on low voltage. Folding the coefficient in at the board's price understates what charging at once pays (at 131.03 EUR per MWh, 0.00171 EUR more per kWh with VAT), so it slightly understates the saving.
