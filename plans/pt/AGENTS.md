# plans/pt/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges.

- **Dynamic contracts:** available, but rare. In ACER and CEER's 2026 retail report (Figures 18 and 15, 2025 data from ERSE, https://www.acer.europa.eu/sites/default/files/documents/Publications/ACER-CEER-2026-Retail-Monitoring.pdf), 92% of Portugal's household contracts have a single fixed price, 8% are time of use or indexed, and dynamic ones are under 1%, though 99% of homes have a smart meter. Suppliers with over 200,000 customers must offer dynamic prices to homes with a smart meter, and those with over 50,000 an indexed price (ERSE's Regulamento n.º 827/2023, article 15, https://www.erse.pt/media/cw5fdm3i/regulamento-827-2023_consolidado.pdf). Offers billing each quarter-hour of the meter's readings (E-REDES's load diagram): Galp Plano Dinâmico, Repsol Tarifa Leve Sem Mais, Coopérnico Base 2.0, G9 Smart Dynamic SPOT 8! and MEO Energia Tarifa Dinâmica (their pages below), and, as tiagofelicia.pt's simulator lists them, EDP Eletricidade Indexada Horária, Alfa Energia ALFA POWER INDEX BTN and EZU Indexada, with Iberdrola and Plenitude in Simples only. G9 moved all its customers to its dynamic offer in July 2026 (DECO PROteste, 24 July 2026). Galp's Plano Flexível spreads the month's use by ERSE's standard profile, and the simulator has offers from EDP, Endesa, Goldenergy, Ibelectra, Luzboa and LUZiGÁS on monthly averages: they don't reward the cheap quarter-hours.
  - The price they bill is OMIE's day-ahead price for Portugal (PMSp, "OMIE PT"), by the quarter-hour in 2026 (Repsol's sheet of 1 September 2026, Galp's and MEO's pages; Coopérnico still says each hour), times ERSE's loss factor for low voltage, which E-REDES sets for each quarter-hour. From October 2025 to September 2026 that factor averaged 1.13 from 00:00 to 07:00, 1.17 from 18:00 to 19:00 and from 22:00 to 23:00, and 1.16 over all hours (tiagofelicia.pt's data, below); ERSE's 2026 averages are 1.1409 off-peak and 1.1689 the rest of the day (Diretiva n.º 1/2026, article 49). With one factor, 1.15, the market price's multiplier with VAT is 1.15 x 1.23 = 1.4145: a vat of 0.4145 in the program.
  - MEO sets a negative energy price to 0 and G9 a negative OMIE price. Prices at or below 0 came in 2.1% of the quarter-hours from 18:00 to 07:00.
- **VAT:** 23% on the kWh a car adds, from October 2025 to September 2026. 6% applies only to a home's first 200 kWh in 30 days (300 for large families), and only up to 6.9 kVA (Lei n.º 38/2024, de 7 de agosto, since 1 January 2025: ERSE's https://www.erse.pt/media/tcsfm4n2/ersexplica_iva-fatura_2025.pdf). Charging at 11 kW needs more than 6.9 kVA, so such a home pays 23% on every kWh. VAT applies to the electricity tax and the social tariff's financing too.
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** 0.00377 with VAT, the same all year:
  - the electricity tax (IEC), 0.00100 without VAT, 0.00123 with it (Coopérnico's sheet below, https://goldenergy.pt/glossario/imposto-especial-sobre-consumo-eletricidade-iec/); homes on the social tariff don't pay it;
  - the social tariff's financing, 0.00207 (2.0666 EUR per MWh) without VAT, 0.00254 with it, which REN bills suppliers on the energy they sell in 2026 (ERSE's Diretiva n.º 12-A/2025, https://files.diariodarepublica.pt/2s/2025/12/250000005/0012900145.pdf). Repsol and Coopérnico bill it per kWh; Galp and MEO may have it in their price.
  - Left out: the power term (EUR a day, with the network's), the audiovisual contribution (2.85 EUR a month, 6% VAT) and the DGEG fee (0.07 EUR a month). No price cap or state support that changes the price by the hour was found; the social tariff (33.8% off in 2026, no IEC) needs at most 6.9 kVA.
- **Supplier's margin:** 0.03444 with VAT (0.02800 without), about the middle of five quarter-hour offers. About 0.017 of it (0.021 with VAT) is REN's regulation charges (encargos de regulação), which every offer passes on in one way or another; the rest is the supplier's own. Each offer's addition per kWh at night, without and with VAT, at a loss factor of 1.13, REN's charges at 0.015 and OMIE's night average, 0.0894, for the shares of the market price:
  - Galp Plano Dinâmico, Ci 0.0191 times losses: 0.02158, 0.02655. Ci covers system services, imbalances, guarantees of origin and the margin, and can change each January, April, July and October (https://www.galp.com/pt/casa/planos-eletricidade-e-gas/flexivel, read 11 October 2026).
  - Repsol Tarifa Leve Sem Mais, QTarifa 0.02109 plus 3% of the market price after losses (FA 1.03): 0.02412, 0.02967 (sheet v20260901, https://www.repsol.pt/content/dam/repsol-portugal/eletricidade-e-g%C3%A1s/tarifas-particulares/repsol-tarifas-eletricidade-leve-sem-mais.pdf). QTarifa has been 0.02109 since June 2025, 0.01479 before.
  - Coopérnico Base 2.0, (k 0.009 + CR + CS) times losses, CR being REN's charges as a three-month average (0.01963 in July 2026) and CS 0.001: 0.02825, 0.03475 (July 2026, https://prd.coopernico.org/storage/files/tarifario.pdf). Members buy three shares, 60 EUR once.
  - G9 Smart Dynamic SPOT 8!, K2 0.0185 (REN's charges, an indicative average) + K3 0.008 + 2% of the market price after losses (K1 1.02): 0.02852, 0.03508 (prices for 13 June - 13 July 2026, https://g9.pt/energia/).
  - MEO Energia Tarifa Dinâmica, K 0.015 + CG times losses, CG being the system's and MEO's own imbalance costs each quarter-hour, not published, taken as 0.015: 0.03195, 0.03930 (sheet of 17 September 2026, https://conteudos.meo.pt/meoenergia/Documentos/ficha-informacao-padronizada-meo-energia.pdf). MEO bills an information service monthly after its campaign.
  - REN's regulation charges averaged 15.95 EUR per MWh over all hours from October 2025 to September 2026, 14.26 from 00:00 to 07:00, 12.64 in October - December 2025 and 17.06 since (tiagofelicia.pt's data, below). Iberdrola's indexed Simples shows Q 0.0250 and an mFRR band of 0.0019 (iberdrola.pt).
- **The plan:** `pt/bi-horaria-diario`. Bi-horária makes night charging pay 0.01943 to the network instead of Simples' 0.07466 with VAT, and its daily cycle has off-peak (vazio) from 22:00 to 08:00 every day of the year (Diretiva n.º 1/2026, article 45), where the weekly cycle's starts at 00:00 on weekdays. Its cheap fee starts at 22:00. No figure says what car owners take: ERSE expects 362,603 Bi-horária customers on the free market in 2026 against 5,224,196 on Simples (https://www.erse.pt/media/5zclblrg/caracteriza%C3%A7%C3%A3o-procura-se-2026.pdf). The plan's prices match 2026's access tariffs, 0.0835 and 0.0158 without VAT (Diretiva n.º 1/2026, Diário da República of 7 January 2026, page 16, https://files.diariodarepublica.pt/2s/2026/01/004000000/0018600288.pdf, and ERSE's s_tarifas_net.xlsx). They held all year: Diretiva ERSE n.º 4/2026 changed only the regulated energy price from 1 October 2026, and the access tariffs in its s_tarifas_net_outubro-2026.xlsx are the same. Until 31 December 2025 they were 0.0830 and 0.0149, 0.10209 and 0.01833 with VAT (Diretiva n.º 2/2025, https://www.erse.pt/media/oiwgcxk5/diretiva-2_2025-tarifas-2025.pdf). Holidays are ordinary days in low voltage: only MAT, AT and MT on the weekly cycle treat them as Sundays (article 45).
- **Prices to download:** OMIE's daily files, as the board downloads them, without an account or key, like https://www.omie.es/es/file-download?parents=marginalpdbc&filename=marginalpdbc_20260101.1. Each line is year;month;day;quarter-hour;Portugal;Spain in EUR per MWh, for a CET day: Lisbon is an hour behind (standard offset 0), so a night from 18:00 to 07:00 is 19:00 to 08:00 CET, across two files. Read Portugal's column ("PT"), which the offers bill: it differed from Spain's in 10.4% of the quarter-hours from October 2025 to September 2026, though both averaged about 73.3 EUR per MWh. Every day from 1 October 2025 to 1 October 2026 downloads; OMIE keeps only a day's latest file, .3 for 30 October 2025 and .2 for 27 November 2025. Before 1 October 2025 the files are hourly.
