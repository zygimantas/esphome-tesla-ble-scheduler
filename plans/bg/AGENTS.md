# plans/bg/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges.

- **Dynamic contracts:** Rare. Nearly every household buys at a regulated price from its end supplier: Electrohold Sales in the west, EVN Bulgaria Elektrosnabdyavane in the south-east, ENERGO-PRO Sales in the north-east. In 2024 that was 4,757,979 of 4,760,505 households, and 2,326 of the rest had a supplier of last resort (EWRC's 2025 report to the European Commission, section 3.2.2: https://www.dker.bg/uploads/2025/EWRC_Report_EC_2025_bg.pdf). ACER counts 100% regulated (https://aegis.acer.europa.eu/chest/dataitems/1001/view). Parliament put off opening the household market indefinitely in May 2025 (https://news.bg/energy/ofitsialno-liberalizatsiyata-na-pazara-na-tok-za-bita-se-otlaga-za-neopredelen-srok.html). EWRC now sets a base value for the energy, and the Electricity System Security Fund pays the end suppliers the difference to what the energy cost them, 24.56 EUR/MWh from July 2026, for their household customers only. From 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027 the energy costs 0.09297 by day and on single-rate meters and 0.04103 at night, without VAT (EWRC's decision Ц-13 of 1 July 2026: https://www.dker.bg/uploads/reshenia/2026/res-c-13-2026.pdf). With the network fees and VAT that's 0.15425 and 0.09192 in ERM Zapad's area, 0.15458 and 0.09226 in ER Yug's and 0.15512 and 0.09280 in ERP Sever's. From 1 July 2025 it was 0.17514 and 0.07546 lev (0.08955 and 0.03858 EUR), 0.14974 and 0.08858 with the fees and VAT in the west. The night is 22:00 - 06:00 from November to March and 23:00 - 07:00 from April to October, every day (https://electrohold.bg/bg/sales/domakinstva/snabdyavane-po-regulirani-ceni/, https://www.energo-pro.bg/bitovi-klienti/ceni-tarifi). A household may leave for a trader and come back later. Traders' exchange-price offers bill IBEX's day-ahead price for Bulgaria, averaged over the month by the site's consumption in each hour or quarter-hour. A household whose meter doesn't record quarter-hours is settled on the network operator's standard load profile (the Electricity Trading Rules, art. 86(2): https://www.dker.bg/uploads/normative_docs/prav_targ_el_en_072025.pdf; ERM_H1 and ERM_H2 in the west: https://ermzapad.bg/bg/za-klienta/svoboden-pazar/smyana-na-dostavchik/standartizirani-tovarovi-profili/), so when the car charges doesn't change its bill. A meter that records them costs 327.37 lev with VAT to fit, or 100.84 lev to reprogram, at ERP Sever (Energy Act art. 120(5), its price list of 28 March 2025: https://www.erpsever.bg/web/files/richeditor/documents/cenorazpisi/2025-03-28-edn-pricelist-in-force-by-28-03-2025-v1.pdf). Even then the regulated night is cheaper: from October 2025 to September 2026 the cheapest quarter-hours of each night (9.1 kWh at 11 kW between 18:00 and 07:00) averaged 0.09766 on the market alone, 0.17869 with the margin below, ERM Zapad's and ESO's fees and VAT, against 0.09192.
- **VAT:** 20% on the whole bill, the standard rate (VAT Act art. 66; the reduced 9% of art. 66a doesn't cover electricity), with no other rate from October 2025 to September 2026. EVN's and ER Yug's price pages apply 20% (https://www.evn.bg/Home/Electricity.aspx, https://www.elyug.bg/Customers/Prices/Bitovi_Klienti.aspx).
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** ESO's access to the transmission network, 0.00018 without VAT and 0.00022 with, and its transmission, 0.00801 and 0.00961 (Ц-13, I.1.1 and I.1.4; the same on ER Yug's and EVN's pages). The obligations to society fee is 0, as the Electricity System Security Fund covers it (Ц-13, I.6). Excise on electricity for a household's use is 0 (Excise Duties and Tax Warehouses Act art. 34a(2), and 0 on EVN's bills), against 2 lev (1.02 EUR) per MWh for businesses. Sum with VAT: 0.00983. From 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026 ESO's fees were 15.22 lev per MWh, 7.78 EUR by EWRC's conversion: 0.00934 with VAT. EWRC's decision Ц-40 of 30 December 2025 kept them, and the network fees, for the first half of 2026. No charge is a share of the bill, and regulated households pay no monthly fee.
- **Supplier's margin:** 0.01882 with VAT (0.01568 without), halfway between the middle two of the four offers for homes that state an adder on EWRC's comparison platform (https://platforma.dker.bg/traders, read 11 October 2026; it shows no dates). Ares Energy's AresEnergy Bitovi: IBEX + 8.80 EUR/MWh. EVN's FLEKS: IBEX + 2.71 EUR/MWh for 6 months, then + 7% but at least 12.94 EUR/MWh, with 2.55 EUR a month per meter, for 24 months, for supply from 1 May 2026; EVN's own page lists it for businesses (https://www.evn.bg/Business/Electricity/BusinessElectricityFree/Tarifen-plan-flex-percent.aspx). Electrohold Sales' EHP Borsa +: IBEX + 18.41 EUR/MWh. Electrohold Trade's optimal: IBEX + 18.82 EUR/MWh. All include balancing. Four more offers for homes give no figure.
- **The plan:** plans/bg/erm-zapad-households. ERM Zapad, the west with Sofia, carried 41% of the electricity distributed in 2024, ER Yug 36% and ERP Sever 22% (EWRC's report). Its fee is the same every hour, as are the other two plans', so there's no cheap fee to start at; only the regulated energy price has a night rate. All three plans match Ц-13 and the operators' own pages from 1 July 2026: ERM Zapad 0.00347 + 0.02391, ER Yug 0.00419 + 0.02347 and ERP Sever 0.00499 + 0.02312 without VAT. From 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026 (decision Ц-25, in EWRC's euros) they were 0.03294, 0.03307 and 0.03460 with VAT, against 0.03286, 0.03319 and 0.03373 now. EWRC changes them every 1 July, and 1 July 2027 starts a new regulatory period. Households pay in euros since 1 January 2026, at 1.95583 lev to the euro (https://economy-finance.ec.europa.eu/euro/eu-countries-and-euro/bulgaria-and-euro_en), with prices shown in lev too until 8 August 2026. The plans are in EUR, and 2025's lev prices convert at that rate.
- **Prices to download:** Energy-Charts' API, without an account or key, in EUR/MWh by quarter-hour from 1 October 2025, complete to 30 September 2026: https://api.energy-charts.info/price?bzn=BG&start=2025-10-01&end=2025-10-31 (Bulgarian dates, the end day included). Take a month at a time: quick repeats get Too Many Requests. Its terms allow private and internal use only. These are IBEX's day-ahead prices for Bulgaria, which dynamic contracts bill: they equal Nord Pool's quarter-hour for quarter-hour on 10 October 2026, and their averages over the CET days of 11 and 12 October 2026, 175.16 and 235.50 EUR/MWh, are IBEX's base prices (https://3e-news.net/bg/pazar-na-elektricheska-energija/elektroenergijni-borsi). ibex.bg answers scripts with a JavaScript check, and Nord Pool answers 401 for days before its last two months.
- **Uncertain:** No count of households on exchange-price contracts newer than 2024's. No offer found bills a household its own quarter-hours: they all average by the site's consumption, which for nearly every household is the standard profile. The platform's adders carry no dates. EVN's page doesn't show the night's hours; Electrohold's and ENERGO-PRO's agree. The 2025 energy prices in euros (0.08955 and 0.03858) are converted here at the fixed rate, as EWRC's decision gives only the network fees in euros.
