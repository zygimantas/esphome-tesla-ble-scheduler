# plans/sk/

Slovakia's research, checked in October 2026, for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp). Start an update here, re-checking the sources under References and the dates under Changes ahead, rather than researching afresh.

## Summary

On the regulated price, ZSE's DD3 without the state's aid, the board's charging cost 508.80 EUR over the year from 1 October 2025: 27% less than charging at once (701.53 EUR), and the same as the car's own schedule from 23:30 (508.80 EUR), which is also its best start, as the market price isn't in the bill. Confidence in the README is High: nearly every home pays a regulated price, cheaper at night.

## Decisions

- **Contract:** the regulated price, as nearly every home pays it: ZSE's DD3 without the state's aid, as not every home gets it. Dynamic contracts (0.1% of homes) cost more than the regulated price and probably give up the aid.
- **Area:** ZSD's, the largest distributor's, with D3 Aktiv, which a home with a charger gets.
- **Plan:** a custom plan of DD3 with ZSD's fees and levies in the rates: low tariff 0.15264 and high 0.21046 EUR per kWh with VAT, the low tariff at 23:30 - 05:30 and 13:30 - 15:30, the hours of most of ZSD's programs. The car's own schedule starts at 23:30, when the low tariff does.
- **Margin and the same every hour:** nothing on top: the custom plan's rates hold the whole price per kWh, ZSD's fees, the levies and VAT included.
- **Left out:** the supplier's monthly fee and D3 Aktiv's fee per ampere and phase, as they don't depend on when the car charges.
- **Market prices:** none, as the market price isn't in the bill: the board can at best match the car's own schedule, and only with a custom plan of the home's hours.

## Findings

- **Who pays the market price:** about 0.1% of homes, as households buy at regulated prices that don't follow the market (Energy price, below). At the end of 2024, 2,541 of 2,468,324 household contracts had a dynamic price. They took 17,124 MWh, 6.7 MWh a contract against 2.4 on fixed prices, so they are big users, likely with cars or heat pumps (a guess). ÚRSO's report says a dynamic price is in current conditions higher than the regulated one ([ÚRSO, October 2025](https://www.urso.gov.sk/data/files/1259_sprava_dynamicke_ceny_elektriny_10_2025_zverejnenie_na_webe.pdf)). In February 2026 ÚRSO said about 27,000 customers of all kinds used dynamic prices in 2025, mostly industry and business ([ÚRSO](https://www.urso.gov.sk/dynamicke-tarify-budu-dobrovolne/)).
- **Meters and billing:** a supplier of over 100,000 metering points must sign a dynamic contract on request when the home has a smart meter (IMS), as ÚRSO's report says (Act 251/2012, § 34(2)(l)). A home with a charger gets the meter from its distributor ([decree 358/2013](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2013/358/20231101.html), § 3(5)). ZSD had smart meters at 243,000 of its low-voltage metering points in 2025 ([ZSD](https://www.skupinazse.sk/documents/30842/zsdis-fs-2025-sk.pdf)). Dynamic contracts bill OKTE's day-ahead price by quarter-hour, weighted by the home's own use in each quarter-hour, plus a markup. DD9, the regulated dynamic rate, bills OKTE's hourly prices, with the average capped at the regulated maximum ([decree 154/2024](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2024/154/20251127.html), § 37).
- **VAT:** 19% on everything on the bill. That's the reduced rate for electricity, CN 2716 00 00 in [Annex 7, point 1](https://static.slov-lex.sk/pdf/prilohy/SK/ZZ/2004/222/20260101_5815198-2.pdf) of the VAT Act ([§ 27(2)](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2004/222/20260101.html)), not the standard 23%. It was 20% until the end of 2024. It didn't change from October 2025 to September 2026: Slov-Lex has no version of the Act between 1 January 2026 and 1 January 2027, and the 2027 one has the same § 27. [ÚRSO's table of the 2026 prices](https://www.urso.gov.sk/ceny-energii-na-rok-2026/) uses 19% too.
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** 0.03975 EUR without VAT, 0.04730 with it, in 2026 in ZSD's area, for D3 Aktiv, which a home with a charger gets ([ZSD's price list](https://www.zsdis.sk/documents/96353/ZSD-Cennik-za-distribuciu-A5_0126_final.pdf), point n). The parts held from January to July 2026 at least, and no later change was found:
  - distribution 0.00396 EUR and distribution losses 0.00747 EUR ([ÚRSO decision 0077/2026/E](https://www.zsdis.sk/documents/96346/0077_2026_E.pdf)), the distribution price including the transmission;
  - system services (TSS) 0.01098 EUR and system operation (TPS) 0.01407 EUR for homes under 1 GWh ([ÚRSO's table](https://www.urso.gov.sk/ceny-energii-na-rok-2026/), [VSD and ZSD](https://www.vse.sk/sdoc/doc/prezentacie/02_Varga_Semanova.pdf));
  - the nuclear fund levy (NJF) 0.00327 EUR, 3.27 EUR/MWh, as in 2025;
  - no excise: households are exempt ([Act 609/2007](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2007/609/20260101.html), § 7(1)(j)), while the rate is 1.32 EUR/MWh for others.

  The parts add up to the end prices in [ZSE's integrated price list](https://www.zse.sk/dokumenty/integrovany-cennik-pre-domacnosti.pdf) and in [SPP's](https://www.spp.sk/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Koncova-cena-dodavky-EE-pre-domacnosti-od-1.7.2026.pdf). The same parts in the other areas: SSD 0.04700 EUR (0.05593 with VAT) from 1 July 2026, VSD 0.04289 EUR (0.05104 with VAT). Fixed, left out: the supplier's 1.50 EUR a month (1.785 with VAT), and D3 Aktiv's 0.1254 EUR per ampere and phase a month, 9.405 EUR (11.19 with VAT) at 3 x 25 A.
- **The distributors:** ZSD is the largest: 1,246,675 metering points in 2025, SSD about 785,000 in 2023 ([SSD](https://www.ssd.sk/buxus/docs/dokumenty/o_nas/vyrocne_spravy/VS%202023%20SK.pdf)), VSD 680,375.
- **Energy price:** nearly all homes buy at the regulated prices. ÚRSO sets a maximum for each rate and supplier ([decision 0048/2026/E for Energetika Slovensko](https://data.urso.gov.sk/CISRES/Agenda.nsf/0/3A73EFC05157F901C1258D37003BB381/$FILE/0048_2026_E_Energetika_Slovensko.pdf)), and the big suppliers charge it. Energetika Slovensko, with ZSE in the west and VSE in the east, is the largest supplier ([annual report](https://www.zse.sk/dokumenty/vyrocna-sprava-spolocnosti-energetika-slovensko-a-s.pdf)). Decree 154/2024 lets DD4, DD5 and DD6 serve a charger (§ 37(4)(d) to (f)). DD3, night current, is the cheapest of the rates that suit a charger: its low tariff (NT) lasts 8 hours at fixed times, with no blocking of appliances, and ZSD pairs it with D3 Aktiv. No statistics on the rates EV owners take were found. Per kWh with VAT, for the ZSE line ([ZSE's price page](https://www.zse.sk/produkty/cena-elektriny)), in the high tariff (VT) and the NT:
  - DD3 without the aid: energy 0.16316 and 0.10534; with the 0.04730 above, 0.21046 and 0.15264, as ZSE's integrated price list shows (0.2105 and 0.1526);
  - DD3 with the aid: energy 0.12599 and 0.06816; with the 0.04730, 0.17329 and 0.11546;
  - DD4, energy only: 0.17168 and 0.12032 without the aid, 0.13451 and 0.08314 with it; DD5 and DD6: 0.18249 and 0.12772, 0.14532 and 0.09054;
  - other suppliers, DD3 energy only: SSE 0.14617 and 0.10173, 0.10899 and 0.06456 with the aid ([SSE](https://www.sse.sk/buxus/docs/dokumenty/domacnosti/2026/Prehlad-cien-dodavky-elektrina-dom-od-1-1-2026.pdf)); SPP 0.15101 and 0.10056, 0.11384 and 0.06338 (SPP's list above);
  - ZSE's EV offer, for homes without the aid only: an NT of 0.0738 for DD3 and 0.0843 for DD4 as published, the VT unchanged ([ZSE's price list D](https://www.zse.sk/dokumenty/cennik-za-dodavku-elektriny-pre-domacnosti-d.pdf)).

  Worked out from these, on a regulated two-band rate the whole gain is charging in the NT: 0.05782 EUR a kWh, about 192 EUR a year for the example's 3,321 kWh, 33% less than charging at once with the aid and 27% less without it.
- **The state's aid:** it takes 31.24 EUR/MWh off every rate and band before VAT, 0.03718 a kWh with it ([government decree 359/2025](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2025/359/20260115.html), § 1(5)), so the gap between VT and NT is 0.05782 either way. It goes to homes that buy at a regulated price and whose average monthly income, divided by a size coefficient (1 for the first person from 14 years, 0.7 for each further one, 0.5 for each child), is under 1,930 EUR ([decree 353/2025](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2025/353/20260701.html), § 8 and annex 1). The government expected about 90% of homes to get it ([startitup.sk](https://www.startitup.sk/fico-tvrdi-ze-energopomoc-zastihne-az-90-domacnosti-vlada-schvalila-navrh-ktory-zastropuje-ceny-energii/)). The suppliers call their dynamic price unregulated ([SSE's notice](https://www.sse.sk/buxus/docs/dokumenty/Informacny_material_k_dynamickej_cene.pdf)), and the aid needs a price regulated by ÚRSO ([Act 260/2025](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2025/260/20260701.html), § 4(2)), so a home that takes one probably gives up the aid.
- **Margins:** what dynamic contracts add to OKTE's price. VSE, a brand of Energetika Slovensko, adds 25.00 EUR/MWh ([VSE](https://www.vse.sk/produkty-a-sluzby/elektrina/dynamicka-tarifa)). Its other brand, ZSE, adds a K agreed in the contract ([ZSE](https://www.zse.sk/dokumenty/informacia-o-dynamickej-cene-elektriny.pdf)), and SSE a variable fee ([SSE, 19 December 2025](https://www.sse.sk/buxus/docs/dokumenty/Informacny_material_k_dynamickej_cene.pdf)): ZSE and SSE publish no amount. Worked out from these, VSE's 25.00 EUR/MWh is 0.02975 EUR a kWh with VAT, if the page's amount is without it. On the year's mean market price, 118.48 EUR/MWh, it comes to 143.48 EUR/MWh without VAT, above the regulated DD2's 123.38, or 92.14 with the aid.
- **October to December 2025:** every home had a capped price ([government decree 384/2024](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2024/384/20250101.html), § 3), with a commodity part of about 61 EUR/MWh (ÚRSO's table above), and there was no aid to ask for. DD3 energy was 0.10448 in the VT and 0.06390 in the NT, 0.15925 and 0.11867 with that quarter's fees. In ZSD's area those were 0.04602 EUR (0.05476 with VAT): distribution 0.00414, losses 0.01029, TSS 0.01242, TPS 0.01590, NJF 0.00327 ([ZSE's integrated price list from 1 July 2025](https://www.zse.sk/dokumenty/integrovany-cennik-pre-domacnosti/integrovany-cennik-pre-domacnosti-2025-07-01_00_00_00.pdf)).
- **The grid fees by the hour:** none vary in the largest area. D3 Aktiv has one distribution price for VT and NT in ZSD ([decision 0077/2026/E](https://www.zsdis.sk/documents/96346/0077_2026_E.pdf)) and in VSD ([price list](https://www.vsds.sk/mdoc/dso.B6000.A/doc/VSD_cennik_distribucie_elektriny-D-2026.pdf)). SSD's D3 charged 0.00401 EUR by day and 0.00060 by night until 30 June 2026, then 0.00927 for both ([decision 0097/2026/E](https://www.ssd.sk/buxus/docs/dokumenty/cenniky_a_cenove_rozhodnutia_urso/0097_2026_E.pdf)). So the network fees need no plan, and they are in the flat part above.
- **The low-tariff hours:** what changes by the hour is the regulated energy price, by the NT hours of the metering point's tariff program (HDO code), which [ZSD lists](https://www.zsdis.sk/Uvod/Online-sluzby/Casy-prepinania-nizkej-a-vysokej-tarify). Its 15 programs for DD3 all give 8 hours a day, on weekends too. Nine have 6 hours from a start between 23:30 and 23:52, plus 2 hours in the afternoon, mostly between 13:30 and 15:50. Four have 22:00 - 06:00. Two have 00:00 - 06:00 plus an afternoon spell. DD3's NT hours are at fixed times (decree 154/2024, § 37(4)(c)), so a custom plan can hold a home's. SSD also fixes D3's intervals, which it publishes on ssd.sk, but may change those of D4 to D8 at any time without notice. An owner would set the car at the NT start, about 23:30 for most programs.
- **Market prices:** OKTE's API, a day a file, in EUR/MWh without VAT, as the board downloads it, like [1 January 2026](https://isot.okte.sk/api/v1/dam/results?deliveryDayFrom=2026-01-01&deliveryDayTo=2026-01-01), without an account or key. All 35,040 quarter-hours from 1 October 2025 to 30 September 2026 are there and final. They equal Energy-Charts' Slovak series ([March 2026](https://api.energy-charts.info/price?bzn=SK&start=2026-03-01T00:00Z&end=2026-04-01T00:00Z)) on every shared quarter-hour of October 2025, January, March and September 2026. Worked out from them, the year's mean is 118.48 EUR/MWh, from -500.00 to 755.42, with 1,088 negative quarter-hours (3.1%), and 134.85 from 18:00 to 07:00. Dynamic contracts bill this price.

## Changes ahead

- **1 January each year:** ÚRSO's new regulated prices and distribution fees, as on 1 January 2026. A distributor's fees can change during the year too, as SSD's did on 1 July 2026.
- **The prices for 2027:** DD8 replaces DD9, with quarter-hour or hourly prices plus at most 3 EUR/MWh ([decree from 1 July 2026](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2024/154/20260701.html), § 37 and § 50a).

## Open questions

- **The dynamic markups:** whether VSE's 25.00 EUR/MWh includes VAT, and what ZSE and SSE ask.
- **Negative prices:** how a dynamic contract bills one: the published formulas have no floor.
- **EV owners:** which rate they really take, and how many pass the aid's income test: the 90% is a forecast, no final count was found, and EV owners may earn more than most (a guess).
- **SSD's and VSD's NT hours:** only ZSD's programs were read.
- **2025's dynamic contracts:** ÚRSO's next report on them, with 2025's counts, wasn't out.

## Notes

- **Custom plans:** DD3's NT hours are at fixed times for each address (The low-tariff hours, above), so a custom plan can hold a home's, as the example's does, and plans/README.md says so since October 2026; SSD may change D4 to D8's at any time.
- **HDO on winter time:** some HDO devices stay on winter time all year, which puts their hours an hour later on the clock in summer. ZSD's page lists the codes 101, 102, 103, 111, 149, 150, 151, 159 and 162, and asks for the device type.
- **D9 in name:** Magna Energia lists DD9 ([ÚRSO decision 0010/2026/E](https://data.urso.gov.sk/CISRES/Agenda.nsf/0/0FD21461BB257CDDC1258D2B003434AB/$FILE/0010_2026_E_MAGNA_ENERGIA_Piestany.pdf)), but the D9 product on its [price page](https://www.magna-energia.sk/domacnosti/ceny-elektriny-a-plynu-na-rok-2026/) is four fixed daily bands.
