# plans/it/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges.

- **Dynamic contracts:** available but rare, and they bill each hour's PUN Index GME, Italy's national price, not IT-North's quarter-hours. In ARERA's survey of 2025 ([Relazione annuale 2026](https://www.arera.it/fileadmin/allegati/relaz_ann/26/RA26_Volume_1.pdf), Tav. 2.61, 2.64 and 2.65), 49% of the 24.3 million homes in the free market have a variable price: 89.1% of them follow the month's average PUN and 7.1% the hourly price, the EU's dynamic contract, so about 3.5% of free-market homes. 69.7% of free-market homes pay one price in every hour. The 3.0 million homes in the Servizio a tutele graduali (until 31 March 2027) pay the month's PUN after the fact, by band for two thirds of them, and the 3.2 million vulnerable ones ARERA's quarterly prices for F1 and F23. The hourly offers come from Duferco Energia (Evergreen Flex, Pianeta Blu Flex, Domus Flex) and about a dozen small suppliers, among the Portale Offerte's [non-comparable offers](https://www.ilportaleofferte.it/portaleOfferte/it/offerte-non-confrontabili.page?commodityType=ELETTRICO): the hour's PUN times 1.10 for grid losses, on the 2G meter's hourly readings. None of them bills quarter-hours. GME has set the PUN Index GME for each quarter-hour since 1 October 2025, as the zones' prices weighted by what each zone buys ([DTF n. 25 MPE](https://www.mercatoelettrico.org/Portals/0/Documents/it-IT/20250101DTF25MPE.pdf)), and its hourly value is the mean of the four (checked for March 2026). Homes pay it all over Italy, whatever their zone. Monthly offers bill the month's average PUN in each band the meter reads: F1 08:00 - 19:00 Monday to Friday; F2 07:00 - 08:00 and 19:00 - 23:00 Monday to Friday and 07:00 - 23:00 on Saturday; F3 the rest, 23:00 - 07:00 and all of Sundays and national holidays (1 and 6 January, Easter Monday, 25 April, 1 May, 2 June, 15 August, 1 November, 8, 25 and 26 December); F23 is F2 and F3. On those the board saves only what charging in F3 saves, as the car's own schedule from 23:00 does.
- **VAT:** 10% for homes, on everything, the excise too (DPR 633/1972, Tabella A, parte III; `iva_c` in the Portale Offerte's [parameters](https://www.ilportaleofferte.it/portaleOfferte/resources/opendata/csv/parametriML/2026_10/PO_Parametri_Mercato_Libero_E_20261011.csv)). Every PUN offer, hourly or monthly, bills the PUN on the metered kWh plus 10% grid losses (`lambda` there; Enel's, Plenitude's and Duferco's terms say so), so with VAT the market price counts 1.21 times.
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** 2026's, without and with VAT:
  - Excise (accisa): 0.02270 and 0.02497 EUR, for any use in homes ([ADM's rates of 6 October 2026](https://www.adm.gov.it/portale/aliquote-accisa-nazionali)). Residents with up to 3 kW are exempt on their first 150 kWh a month, which a home that charges a car most nights doesn't keep.
  - System charges: ASOS 0.02866 EUR from January to June and 0.03152 from July to December, ARIM 0.00164 all year ([ARERA](https://www.arera.it/area-operatori/prezzi-e-tariffe/oneri-generali-di-sistema-e-ulteriori-componenti), deliberations 588/2025, 98/2026, 227/2026 and 343/2026): 0.03172 EUR on average, 0.03490 with VAT.
  - Dispatching (CDISPD), Terna's dispatching and capacity costs, which free-market contracts pass on at ARERA's monthly value ([ARERA's table](https://www.arera.it/fileadmin/area_operatori/prezzi_e_tariffe/Corrispettivi_libero_elettrico_domestico_2026.xlsx)): 0.01507 to 0.03846 EUR from January to September 2026, the highest in July, 0.02090 on average, 0.02300 with VAT.
  - Together 0.07533 EUR, 0.08286 with VAT. The plan has the grid's own charges per kWh. Fixed, left out: σ1 23.04 EUR a year, σ2 23.52 EUR and UC6 0.19880 EUR per kW a year, DISPbt 1.107 EUR a year, the supplier's fee, and ASOS's 88.75 to 95.09 EUR a year for second homes. The bonus sociale is a fixed daily discount for low incomes, with 115 EUR more in 2026 (decree-law 21/2026, which also cut ASOS for businesses only). No price cap applies in the free market.
  - From October to December 2025: the same excise, ASOS 0.02968, ARIM 0.00165 and dispatching 0.01692 (the Portale Offerte's figure for that quarter): 0.07094 EUR, 0.07803 with VAT.
- **Supplier's margin:** 0.02420 EUR with VAT (0.02200 without), the middle of the big suppliers' offers for homes in the Portale Offerte's [open data](https://www.ilportaleofferte.it/portaleOfferte/it/open-data.page) of 11 October 2026, all on the month's PUN: [Enel Flex Luce](https://www.enel.it/it-it/offerte-luce/enel-flex-luce) 0.02000 EUR, 144 EUR a year and 2% of the PUN for imbalances, until 22 October 2026; [Plenitude Trend Casa Luce](https://eniplenitude.com/offerta/casa/gas-e-luce/trend) 0.02200 and 144 EUR, 16 September to 14 October 2026; [Edison Dynamic Luce](https://www.edisonenergia.it/edison/casa/luce/dynamic-luce) 0.02300 above 2,200 kWh a year (0.01800 below) and 99 EUR, 24 September to 14 October 2026; [A2A Smart Casa Luce](https://casa.a2aenergia.eu/smart-casa) 0.02500 and 114 EUR, 1 to 28 October 2026; Octopus Flex 0.00880 and 72 EUR. The median of all 1,413 such offers is 0.02365 and 139 EUR a year. The hourly ones, in October 2026: Duferco's [Evergreen Flex](https://dufercoenergia.com/wp-content/uploads/CTE/EE_EVERGREEN_FLEX.pdf) 0.00770 and 84 EUR; its [Domus Flex](https://dufercoenergia.com/wp-content/uploads/CTE/EE_DOMUS_FLEX.pdf) none and 84 EUR plus an 84 EUR service, for homes using up to 5,000 kWh a year; the small suppliers' 0.00000 to 0.08790 and 143 to 275 EUR. On 1 October 2025 the big offers above were 0.00880 to 0.02600, and the median of all 1,025 such offers 0.02640.
- **The plan:** `it/td`, ARERA's TD tariff, which every home pays for the grid: one fee per kWh in every hour, so no cheap fee starts. F1, F2 and F3 are the supplier's. Its 0.01620 EUR with VAT is 2026's σ3, 0.01190 (deliberation 575/2025, on the page its first line links), with UC3 0.00276 and UC6 0.00007 (588/2025, kept for every quarter of 2026) and 10% VAT, as ARERA's own [table of 2026](https://www.arera.it/fileadmin/area_operatori/prezzi_e_tariffe/E2026-smt.xlsx) adds them: it matches. From October to December 2025 it was 0.01487 with VAT (σ3 0.01189, UC3 0.00156). A car's own schedule would start at 23:00, where F3 starts. ARERA's trial for cars (541/2020, extended by 22/2025 to 30 June 2027, joining until 30 June 2026) lets a home with 2 to 4.5 kW draw up to 6 kW in F3 at no extra cost.
- **Prices to download:** SMARD's IT-North, as the board downloads it, a week a file from Monday 00:00 German time. It equals GME's Nord zone to the cent (checked for a week of October 2025 and one of July 2026): https://www.smard.de/app/chart_data/255/DE/255_DE_quarterhour_1759096800000.json is 29 September to 5 October 2025, and https://www.smard.de/app/chart_data/255/DE/index_quarterhour.json lists the weeks. GME's PUN has no plain link: its site's API answers with the cookie and the `__RequestVerificationToken` of https://www.mercatoelettrico.org/it-it/Home/Esiti/Elettricita/MGP/Esiti/PUN, sent as the headers RequestVerificationToken, ModuleId 530 and TabId 48, as in https://www.mercatoelettrico.org/DesktopModules/GmeEsitiPrezziME/API/item/GetMEPrezzi?DataInizio=20251001&DataFine=20251031&Granularita=h&Mercato=MGP&Zona=PUN&Tipologia=PUN (`Granularita=qh` for quarter-hours, `Zona=NORD&Tipologia=PrezziZonali` for IT-North). From October 2025 to September 2026 the PUN averaged 0.13761 EUR per kWh and IT-North 0.13811, and 76% of quarter-hours were within 0.00100 of each other. Each night's four cheapest quarter-hours by IT-North (9.1 kWh at 11 kW, 18:00 to 07:00) cost only 0.00030 more at the PUN than the four cheapest by the PUN. Billing by the hour matters more: their market price averaged 0.12285 EUR at each quarter-hour's PUN but 0.12854 at each hour's, against 0.16138 for charging at once and 0.14050 from 23:00 at the hourly PUN (0.15879 and 0.14243 by the quarter-hour). IT-North's hourly means come within 0.00090 of the hourly PUN's figures.
- **Uncertain:** whether ARERA's 7.1% counts all homes on a variable price or only 2025's new contracts; whether CDISPD is charged on the metered kWh, as assumed here, or with the 10% losses too; ARERA's monthly CDISPD for October to December of 2025 and 2026 (the Portale Offerte's quarterly figures, 0.01692 and 0.01678, stand in); the exact taper of the excise exemption; and how many homes can charge at 11 kW, as most have 3 kW and some 4.5 or 6.
