# plans/si/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges.

- **Dynamic contracts:** rare. The Energy Agency (AGEN-RS) counted 2,259 households on them at four suppliers in 2025, 0.26% of the 883,278 households and 0.24% of their energy, up from 385 in 2024 ([report for 2025](https://www.agen-rs.si/-/porocilo-o-stanju-na-podrocju-energetike-v-sloveniji-v-letu-2025), 1 July 2026, pages 29 and 159 to 163). Bisol Energija supplied 71% of that energy, ECE 21%, SunContract 8% and GEN-I 1% (rounded). Households mostly buy at flat supplier price lists (Energy price, below), and only 628 had fixed-price contracts.
  - Offers in September 2026: Bisol, ECE, Elektro energija, Energija plus, GEN-I and SunContract ([ZPS's table](https://www.zps.si/aktualne-vsebine/trzni-pregledi/pregled-cen-elektricne-energije-in-zemeljskega-plina-152), checked 2 September 2026), and Petrol since 1 July 2026. They bill BSP SouthPool's day-ahead price plus a fixed markup and need a meter that reads every 15 minutes, as over 90% of Slovenian meters do. Bisol also takes homes without one and spreads their use over the national load curve, so shifting gains nothing there.
  - Bisol, ECE, GEN-I and SunContract bill the hourly index SIPXhourly, which BSP defines as the average of the hour's 15-minute prices ([BSP's definition](https://web.archive.org/web/20251206130833/https://www.bsp-southpool.com/sipx.html)), times the hour's use ([ECE's terms](https://www.ece.si/app/uploads/2026/07/SPLOSNI-POGOJI-ECE-FLEKS_1.9.2026.pdf) spell it out). So the board's choice of quarter-hours within an hour changes nothing on their bills. Only Petrol bills each quarter-hour at its own price ([terms](https://www.petrol.si/binaries/content/assets/www/2026/dokumenti/ee/pogodbeni-pogoji-za-dobavo-elektricne-energije-po-dinamicnih-cenah-1-01-2026.pdf)).
  - SunContract bills hours at or below 0 as 0. The rest, by ZPS's check, pass negative prices on.
  - A scratch run of the example's method on the numbers below (margin 0.01586, other charges 0.00300): the board's costs were 30.0% less than charging at once, 21.8% less than the car's schedule from 22:00 and 4.3% less than the best fixed start, with each quarter-hour at its own price, and 27.9%, 16.8% and 0.0% less with each billed at its hour's average.
- **VAT:** 22%, all year ([FURS](https://www.fu.gov.si/en/taxes_and_other_duties/areas_of_work/value_added_tax_vat/) gives the general rate, and every 2026 price list here shows it). The temporary 9.5% of September 2022 to May 2023 is over. The government may set 9.5% again on electricity by decree for up to nine months under ZIURS (passed 11 May 2026, [IUS-INFO](https://www.iusinfo.si/medijsko-sredisce/kolumne/ziurs-katere-novosti-prinasa-novi-interventni-zakon-323082)), but the law waits on a referendum and wasn't in force on 11 October 2026. VAT is charged on everything below, the excise included: the ministry's Q1 2026 price adds up that way.
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** 0.00246 EUR without VAT, 0.00300 with it (0.0030012 before rounding), unchanged from October 2025 to September 2026. A [2026 price guide](https://www.varcevanje-energije.si/aktualne-novice/cena-elektrike-2020-drasticno-navzdol/) lists all three, with VAT as 0.0018666, 0.000976 and 0.0001586:
  - the excise (trošarina), 0.00153 EUR without VAT, 0.00187 with it: 1.53 EUR per MWh for homes since 1 August 2022 ([Uradni list 99/2022](https://www.uradni-list.si/_pdf/2022/Ur/u2022099.pdf), article 1 point 7). The six amendments from November 2025 to June 2026 change only fuels, and the [consolidated text](https://www.racunovodstvo.net/zakonodaja/uredba-o-dolocitvi-zneska-trosarine-za-energente-in-elektricno-energijo/1-clen) valid from 16 June 2026 still has 1.53. The ministry's [Q1 2026 prices](https://www.energetika-portal.si/nc/novica/n/cene-elektricne-energije-v-prvem-cetrtletju-2026/) show it too;
  - the energy efficiency contribution (URE), 0.00080 EUR without VAT, 0.00098 with it, the same since 2017 ([energy portal](https://www.energetika-portal.si/podrocja/energetika/prihranki-energije/prispevek-za-energetsko-ucinkovitost/));
  - the market operator's contribution (Borzen), 0.00013 EUR without VAT, 0.00016 with it ([Borzen](https://borzen.si/Portals/0/Trg%20z%20elektriko/Dokumenti/Financiranje%20GJS.pdf)).
- **Fixed per month, left out:**
  - the OVE and SPTE contribution is per kW of billing power, not per kWh: 0.77562 EUR per kW a month without VAT (0.94626 with it) from 1 July 2025, half of it from 1 November 2025 to 28 February 2026, the full rate again from 1 March 2026 ([energy portal](https://www.energetika-portal.si/podrocja/energetika/prispevek-za-obnovljive-vire/)). ZSROVE-1 ([Uradni list 112/2025](https://www.uradni-list.si/glasilo-uradni-list-rs/vsebina/2025-01-3977)) makes it a charge per kWh, but only from a new decree, due by the end of 2026. The estimate behind the law was 0.00845 EUR without VAT ([Žurnal24](https://www.zurnal24.si/pod-streho/varcna-hisa/nov-obracun-prispevka-na-racunu-za-elektriko-kdo-bo-placal-vec-in-kdo-manj-448027), 23 October 2025);
  - the network charge's power part and the suppliers' monthly fees, 0.99 to 3.62 EUR with VAT.
  - The power part does depend on when the car charges. A block's agreed power is set from its five highest quarter-hour peaks in a past year ([act of 29 September 2025](https://www.agen-rs.si/documents/10926/554036/Sprejeti-akt_20251006/893bdb24-a92b-4596-a044-df93eb81b186), article 12), and in 2026 a kW costs 3.82301 EUR a month in block 1, 1.09230 in block 2, 0.28902 in block 3, 0.02436 in block 4 and 0.00245 in block 5 (user group 0, without VAT). An 11 kW car charging in an evening block would add far more than one at night. The example doesn't count it (issue #82).
- **Energy price:** the margin is 0.01586 EUR with VAT (0.01300 without): Bisol's, the supplier of 71% of the household dynamic energy, and near the mean of the seven offers, 0.01285 without VAT. Markups without and with VAT, and monthly fees:
  - Bisol Dinamični 0.01300 and 0.01586, 1.63 EUR (1.99 with VAT) ([Bisol](https://www.bisol-energija.si/dinamicni-gospodinjstva), 11 October 2026);
  - ECE Fleks 0.01400 and 0.01708, 1.21 EUR with VAT;
  - Elektro energija Dinamična oskrba 0.01199 and 0.01463, 3.62 EUR with VAT, 0.99 less with an e-bill;
  - GEN-I Dinamični 0.01199 and 0.01463, from 2 October 2024, 2.97 EUR (3.62 with VAT), 1.97 with an e-bill ([GEN-I](https://www.gen-i.si/dom/elektricna-energija/ceniki-in-akcije/gen-i-dinamicni-gospodinjski-odjemalci/));
  - SunContract Dinamika 0.01200 and 0.01464, 2.43 EUR with VAT;
  - Energija plus Dinamične cene 0.01500 and 0.01830, no fee given;
  - Petrol 0.01195 and 0.01458 from 1 July 2026, 0.01295 and 0.01580 in its terms from 1 January 2026, 1.98 EUR (2.42 with VAT) ([price list](https://www.petrol.si/binaries/content/assets/www/2026/dokumenti/ee/dinamicni-cenik_go-mpo-odjem_1_7_2026.pdf)).

  ZPS's tables of 3 March, 2 June and 2 September 2026 have the same markups. AGEN-RS says markups are fixed, 6.5 to 14 EUR per MWh, or 6 to 10% of the price.

  No cap or regulated energy price exists: the government's last cap (VT 0.08400, MT 0.07000 and ET 0.07700 EUR per kWh without VAT) ended on 28 February 2025 ([ministry](https://www.energetika-portal.si/podrocja/energetika/ukrepi-za-omilitev-draginje-na-podrocju-energetike/elektricna-energija/)). A household on a supplier's price list pays a flat price: in September 2026 ECE's and Energija plus's were VT 0.12479, MT 0.10285 and ET 0.11378 EUR per kWh without VAT (0.15224, 0.12548 and 0.13881 with it), as in [ZPS's table](https://www.zps.si/aktualne-vsebine/trzni-pregledi/pregled-cen-elektricne-energije-in-zemeljskega-plina-152). VT is workdays from 06:00 to 22:00, MT the rest, weekends and holidays included (annex 2 of the [act of 29 September 2025](https://www.agen-rs.si/documents/10926/554036/Sprejeti-akt_20251006/893bdb24-a92b-4596-a044-df93eb81b186), in its text for 2027). The average energy price in Q1 2026 was 0.1175 EUR per kWh without VAT.
- **The plan:** `si/omreznina`, the energy part of the network charge for user group 0, homes on a low-voltage feeder. It is one national price list, the same in every distribution area. Its prices match the AGEN-RS act of 15 December 2025 ([Uradni list 105/2025](https://www.uradni-list.si/glasilo-uradni-list-rs/vsebina/2025-01-3592/akt-o-dolocitvi-tarifnih-postavk-za-omreznine-elektrooperaterjev)), valid from 1 March to 31 December 2026: transmission and distribution together, without VAT, 0.02217, 0.01998, 0.01717, 0.01805 and 0.01299 for blocks 1 to 5, and with 22% VAT the 0.02705, 0.02438, 0.02095, 0.02202 and 0.01585 in the file. Its hours match [AGEN-RS's table for 2026](https://www.uro.si/prenova-omre%C5%BEnine/%C4%8Dasovni-bloki/2026) (its one-column picture labels 14:00 - 16:00 on a lower-season weekend or holiday as 5, against 4 in the other two tables and in the plan).
  - The night block starts at 22:00 on every kind of day: block 3 on workdays from November to February, 4 on workdays from March to October and on weekends and holidays from November to February, 5 on weekends and holidays from March to October. The car's own schedule would start at 22:00. On workdays from March to October block 3 (06:00 - 07:00, 14:00 - 16:00 and 20:00 - 22:00) costs 0.00107 less per kWh than the night's block 4, but the night has almost no fee per kW.
  - The prices changed in the year. From 1 January 2025 to 28 February 2026 the energy part was flatter: 0.01998, 0.01833, 0.01809, 0.01855 and 0.01873 without VAT, 0.02438, 0.02236, 0.02207, 0.02263 and 0.02285 with it ([Uradni list 97/2024](https://www.uradni-list.si/_pdf/2024/Ur/u2024097.pdf) and [uro.si](https://www.uro.si/prenova-omre%C5%BEnine/tarifne-postavke)). Using the file's 2026 prices for October 2025 to February 2026 understates the board's cost for those 151 nights by about 1% (a scratch run: 197.91 EUR, against 199.72 with the old prices). New hours and prices start on 1 January 2027.
  - Easter Monday 2026 (6 April) isn't in the file, which has 2027's date: one night. The page of the file's first line shows its prices as pictures; the same numbers are in Uradni list 105/2025.
- **State support and other rules:** none on the energy price. From 10 April to 10 October 2026 suppliers needed the government's consent to raise retail prices for homes ([Uradni list 227/2026](https://pisrs.si/api/uradni-list/objava/u20260227.pdf)), which caps nothing. The temporary cuts were per kW: the OVE and SPTE contribution at half for November 2025 to February 2026, and block 1's power price at 50% for November and December 2025 and January and February 2026. No special tariff for electric cars turned up. The one supplier price with fixed hours is GEN-I Aktivni since 6 February 2026: without VAT 0.17990 EUR from 18:00 to 21:00, 0.11790 from 21:00 to 09:00 and 16:00 to 18:00, and from 09:00 to 16:00 0.03490 (March to October) or 0.08990 (winter) ([GEN-I](https://www.gen-i.si/dom/elektricna-energija/ceniki-in-akcije/aktivni-cenik-elektrike-za-dom/)). The network blocks treat Saturdays, Sundays and work-free holidays alike. The law of 2 October 2026 that adds 7 August as a holiday makes it one that isn't work-free ([Uradni list 1567/2026](https://pisrs.si/api/uradni-list/objava/u20261567.pdf)), so the plan's dates stand.
- **Prices to download:** SMARD's series 260, a week a file from Monday 00:00 local time, in euros per MWh, like https://www.smard.de/app/chart_data/260/DE/260_DE_quarterhour_1766962800000.json (the week of 29 December 2025). All 35,040 quarter-hours from 1 October 2025 to 30 September 2026 are there, none missing. Its 2025 average, 104.64 EUR per MWh, and its 08:00 - 20:00 average, 98.94, are the figures AGEN-RS's report gives for BSP's day-ahead prices in 2025. It is BSP's SIPXquarterly, the day-ahead price of each quarter-hour: Petrol bills it, the other suppliers its hourly average. Energy-Charts' API (bzn=SI) serves the same numbers, but it republishes SMARD's, so it checks nothing. Nord Pool has no area for Slovenia, and BSP's own data is paid after 60 days.
- **Uncertain:** how SIPXhourly weights the four quarter-hours (BSP shows its formula as a picture; a plain average is assumed); ZPS's table is the only source for ECE's, Elektro energija's, Energija plus's and SunContract's markups, and for SunContract's zero floor; the markups before March 2026 (ZPS's tables of earlier months weren't reachable, while GEN-I's and SunContract's lists date from 2024); whether Elektro energija and Energija plus bill by the hour, as GEN-I does; E 3's offer (none found); whether the network blocks' hours move with summer time (the acts give clock hours only, as the plan does); the caps on GEN-I's and Elektro energija's offers, 220 EUR per MWh, and Bisol Dinamični+'s 197, which the example doesn't model and which would lower evening costs (4.65% of the year's quarter-hours were above 220); and the number of dynamic households in 2026.
