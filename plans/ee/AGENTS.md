# plans/ee/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges. Prices are in euros per kWh.

- **Dynamic contracts:** Common. Every big supplier sells an open-ended exchange-price package (börsipakett), which can be left at 14 days' notice. In June 2026, 60% of Alexela's customers had chosen fixed prices, a share that grew during the year (ERR, 7 August 2026, https://www.err.ee/1610104690/ulevaade-kas-valida-talveks-fikseeritud-elektrihind-voi-borsipakett). Another 61,191 of 659,290 households (9%) were on universal service in 2025, which costs the month's weighted average (Konkurentsiamet's report for 2025, tables 17 and 31, https://www.konkurentsiamet.ee/sites/default/files/documents/2026-09/Elektri-%20ja%20gaasituru%20aruanne%202025_viimane.pdf). Since 1 October 2025 the packages bill Nord Pool's EE day-ahead price for each quarter-hour: Enefit, Elektrum and Alexela say so on their pages, and Alexela's terms for Vali ise define it. Most homes still have hourly meters, though: 110,000 of Elektrilevi's 700,000 meters measured quarter-hours in January 2026 (ERR, 22 January 2026, https://www.err.ee/1609918682/elektrilevil-votab-uute-arvestite-paigaldamine-arvatust-kauem-aega). For the rest, Elektrilevi splits each hour's use equally over its four quarter-hours (https://elektrilevi.ee/et/blogi/mida-pean-teadma-15-minuti-mootmisest, 6 April 2026; Elenger's FAQ says the same), so they pay the hour's average of the four prices.
- **VAT:** 24% on every line of both bills, all year. It's Estonia's standard rate since 1 July 2025, and electricity has no reduced rate (EMTA, https://emta.ee/ariklient/maksud-ja-tasumine/kaibemaks/kaibemaksumaarad-ja-maksuvaba-kaive). Elektrilevi's price list and Konkurentsiamet's report for 2025 apply it too. Nothing changed from October 2025 to September 2026; it was 22% before July 2025.
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** Four charges in 2026, each given without and with VAT. The renewable energy fee (taastuvenergia tasu), 0.00840 and 0.01042, on Elektrilevi's bill, the same as in 2025 (Elektrilevi's price list from 1 June 2026, page 7, https://public-docs.elektrilevi.ee/2/7/vorguteenuse_kehtiv_hinnakiri_71ed51b53d.pdf; its news of 3 December 2025, https://elektrilevi.ee/en/uudised/ulevaade-2026-aasta-vorguteenuse-arvest-lisandub-varustuskindluse-tasu-taastuvenergia-tasu-ei-muutu). The electricity excise (elektriaktsiis), 0.00210 and 0.00260, since 1 May 2025, as the rise planned for 1 May 2026 was cancelled (the same price list; https://elektrilevi.ee/et/blogi/millest-koosneb-elektriarve; KPMG, https://kpmg.com/ee/et/insights/2026/04/infokuller.html). The security of supply fee (varustuskindluse tasu), 0.00758 and 0.00940, on Elektrilevi's bill from 1 January 2026 (https://www.elering.ee/varustuskindluse-tasu; the same price list). The balancing capacity cost (tasakaalustamisvõimsuse kulu), 0.00373 and 0.00463, on the supplier's bill from 1 January 2026, the same all year (https://www.elering.ee/tasakaalustamisvoimsuse-kulu; ERR, 31 October 2025, https://www.err.ee/1609844289/uus-tasu-elektriarvel-saab-olema-3-73-eurot-megavatt-tunni-kohta; Sunly's page lists it too). Sum: 0.02181 without VAT, 0.02704 with. From October to December 2025 only the first two applied, 0.01302 with VAT: the other two are new in 2026. Left out: Elektrilevi's monthly fee by main fuse and the suppliers' monthly fees. Homes up to 63 A pay no fee per kW.
- **Supplier's margin:** 0.00500 with VAT (0.00403 without). It's Enefit's, the biggest supplier's, and the middle of five big suppliers' open-ended exchange-price packages, all checked on 11 October 2026 and given with VAT. Enefit's Muutuv, 0.00500 (0.00403 without), 2.05 EUR a month (https://www.enefit.ee/et/era/elekter/elektrileping-ja-paketid). Alexela's Börsihinnaga elekter, 0.00470 (0.00379), 2.02 EUR a month (https://www.alexela.ee/et/elekter). Elektrum's Börsi Klõps, 0.00533 (0.00430), 1.91 EUR a month, from elektrihind.ee, the comparison site Konkurentsiamet certifies (https://elektrihind.ee/paketid/), as Elektrum shows prices only after login. Elenger's (Eesti Gaas) Muutuv, 0.00670 (0.00540), no monthly fee (https://elenger.ee/kodukliendile/elekter/). Sunly's, no margin but 3 - 9 EUR a month by the last year's use, 6 EUR for 6,001 - 12,000 kWh (https://sunly.ee/elekter/era). Konkurentsiamet's report for 2025 took a margin of 0.00500 without VAT (0.00620 with) for its example bills.
- **The plan:** ee/elektrilevi-vork-2. Elektrilevi runs the grid in 93% of Estonia. For a house with a 25 A main fuse, common where a car charges at 11 kW, its package page suggests Võrk 2 from 1,400 to 9,300 kWh a year and Võrk 4 above, assuming equal day and night use (6,300 kWh with 16 A; https://elektrilevi.ee/et/vorguleping/vorgupaketid/eramu). Households used 3,030 kWh on average in 2025 (Konkurentsiamet), so a house that doesn't heat with electricity likely stays under 9,300 with the car's 3,300. The car's use, all at night, also pushes the point where Võrk 4 pays past 10,000 kWh, as Võrk 4 saves less by night than by day. Above that, Võrk 4 (ee/elektrilevi-vork-4, with the same hours) is the likely one. Võrk 2's night fee, 0.04352 against 0.07527 by day, starts at 22:00 on workdays and applies all day at weekends and on public holidays: the car's own schedule would start at 22:00. Its prices match 2026's. The price list from 1 June 2026 (Konkurentsiamet's decision 7-3/2026-028 of 2 March 2026) has the same fees per kWh as the one for 1 August 2025 - 31 May 2026 (https://public-docs.elektrilevi.ee/2/7/vorguteenuse_hinnakiri_01_08_2025_kuni_31_05_2026_dd157d3c88.pdf); only the monthly fees rose on 1 June 2026. The plans carry 2027's Good Friday (26 March), so a run over 2026 takes Thursday 26 March as a holiday and Friday 3 April, 2026's Good Friday, as a workday.
- **Prices to download:** Elering's dashboard API republishes Nord Pool's day-ahead prices without an account or key, a month a request, in EUR per MWh without VAT. Since 1 October 2025 it gives quarter-hours, Estonia's under data.ee, with UTC timestamps in seconds and the slot at `end` included: https://dashboard.elering.ee/api/nps/price?start=2026-01-01T00:00:00.000Z&end=2026-02-01T00:00:00.000Z. All twelve months from October 2025 to September 2026 are complete, and a day checked against Nord Pool's own EE prices, which the packages bill, matched exactly. The monthly files test/savings_example.cpp downloads for Lithuania already hold them. Tallinn keeps Vilnius's clock (UTC+2, +3 in summer).
- **Uncertain:** No national share of households on exchange-price packages was found; Alexela's is one supplier's. The margins are October 2026's. Earlier in the year they may have differed, though Konkurentsiamet's monthly reviews show the cheapest exchange package at 0.00670 on 21 May, 0.00600 on 29 July and 0.00400 on 7 August 2026, with VAT. Elektrilevi publishes no split of its customers by package, so the plan rests on its own advice. The example prices each quarter-hour, while a home with an hourly meter pays the hour's average, so the board saves it slightly less. Elektrilevi says all meters measure quarter-hours by 2031, while ERR reported in January 2026 that the government moved the deadline for new meters to 2035. No state support, price cap or special tariff for charging at home was found for the year. Enefit's Hinnalaega börsipakett, at the market price between a floor of 0.05990 and a cap of 0.21000 with VAT for 12 months, is a supplier's product. Ahead: the balancing capacity cost falls to 0.00297 without VAT on 1 January 2027 (ERR, 26 June 2026, https://www.err.ee/1610063971/elering-vahendab-jargmisest-aastast-tasakaalustamistasu), and Elering sets the renewable energy and security of supply fees for 2027 by 1 December 2026.
