# plans/lu/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges.

- **Dynamic contracts:** Nearly every home can have one, but few do. A supplier with more than 15,000 customers must offer one (law of 9 June 2023). The Encevo group, with 92.4% of the electricity homes bought in 2024, sells one product under four names: dynamic naturstroum home from Enovos, Leo, Nordenergie and Steinergy. It bills EPEX's DE-LU day-ahead price plus a commission, per quarter-hour since 1 June 2026 and per hour before. SUDenergie's Mäi Stroum dynamic, since 1 May 2026, bills the same price per quarter-hour. Sudstroum's TERRA DYNAMIC bills it per hour, and its own page calls the offer unattractive. The ILR's comparator, [calculix.lu](https://www.calculix.lu), lists just these. It lists no offer from Electris, now in the Encevo group, or from Eida. Energy Revolt's Bee Flexible has fixed prices for four time bands, so it isn't dynamic. Most homes stay with their incumbent on a price the supplier can change at 30 days' notice. At the end of 2024 that was 96.0% of household contracts, and 4.0% had a price fixed for a term. Only 1.0% of customers switched supplier in 2024. The first dynamic offer came in July 2024, and no count of dynamic contracts is published. The ILR judged that offer's 7.5 ct margin a way to meet the legal duty rather than to win customers ([ILR's report for 2024, November 2025](https://www.ceer.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Benchmark-Report-2025-base-2024_final.pdf), pages 8 and 53 - 62). Negative prices are passed on.
- **VAT:** 8% on the whole bill, market price included, in 2025 and 2026. The customs administration's [2026 excise rates](https://douanes.public.lu/content/dam/douanes/fr/accises/taux-accises-lu-01012026.pdf) give 8% VAT for electricity, and every supplier sheet quotes prices without VAT (8%).
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:**
  - Electricity tax (taxe électricité), category A (up to 25,000 kWh a year): 0.00100 without VAT, 0.00108 with VAT. That's 1.00 EUR/MWh in the [2026 excise rates](https://douanes.public.lu/content/dam/douanes/fr/accises/taux-accises-lu-01012026.pdf), unchanged on [1 July 2026](https://douanes.public.lu/content/dam/douanes/fr/accises/taux-accises-lu-01072026.pdf) and the same in 2025.
  - Contribution to the compensation mechanism, category A: -0.00100 without VAT, -0.00108 with VAT, from 1 January to 31 July 2026 ([Règlement ILR/E25/40](https://legilux.public.lu/eli/etat/leg/rilr/2025/12/19/a624/jo)). The state now pays for the mechanism. From 1 August 2026 it's -0.03710 without VAT, -0.04007 with VAT ([Règlement ILR/E26/28](https://legilux.public.lu/eli/etat/leg/rilr/2026/07/16/a353/jo)). That carries the [Resilienzpak 2026 subsidy](https://meco.gouvernement.lu/fr/domaines-activites/energie/marches-de-lenergie/subvention-electricite.html) of 4 ct with VAT, for homes under 25,000 kWh a year, until 31 December 2026. In 2025 it was -0.03760 without VAT, -0.04061 with VAT ([Règlement ILR/E24/56](https://legilux.public.lu/eli/etat/leg/rilr/2024/12/19/a609/jo)).
  - Sum with VAT: 0.00000 from 1 January to 31 July 2026, the value to use for 2026. It's -0.03899 from 1 August 2026, and it was -0.03953 in October - December 2025. Enovos' sheets of [1 January 2026](https://www.enovos.lu/fr/?wpdmdl=897852), [1 August 2026](https://www.enovos.lu/fr/?wpdmdl=901309) and [2025](https://www.enovos.lu/fr/?wpdmdl=896078) show the same, and so do SUDenergie's sheets.
  - Altogether, on the Encevo group's dynamic contract, a kWh cost 0.07668 with VAT on top of the market price from January to July 2026 (plan, margin and sum). It cost 0.03769 from August 2026, and 0.09104 in October - December 2025.
  - Left out: the network's fixed fee by reference power, 7.42 EUR a month without VAT at 3 kW and 19.61 at 12 kW. Also left out: metering at 5.72 EUR a month up to 50 A, and the supplier's monthly fee. The supplement of 0.07650 without VAT, 0.08262 with VAT, on the kWh above the reference power in each quarter-hour depends on power, not the hour (#82).
- **Supplier's margin:** 0.02160 with VAT (0.02000 without), the commission of both dynamic offers that fit:
  - The Encevo group's dynamic naturstroum home charges 0.02000 per kWh plus 13.00 EUR a month, from 1 January 2026, with 2.50 EUR off for e-mailed bills and direct debit. It's per quarter-hour from 1 June 2026 ([Enovos' sheet of 1 January 2026](https://www.enovos.lu/fr/?wpdmdl=897428), [of 1 June 2026](https://www.enovos.lu/fr/?wpdmdl=900486)). Nordenergie's and Steinergy's sheets are the same. In 2025 the commission was 0.04500 without VAT, 0.04860 with VAT ([sheet](https://www.enovos.lu/fr/?wpdmdl=897288)), and from July 2024 it was 0.07500.
  - [SUDenergie's Mäi Stroum dynamic](https://www.sudenergie.lu/fileadmin/documents/Electricite/Consommation/Residentiel/SUDenergie_Conditions_tarifaires_de_fourniture_electricite_residentiel_MaeiStroum_dynamic_20260501.pdf) charges 0.02000 per kWh plus 10.00 EUR a month, with 1.50 EUR off, from 1 May 2026. It's unchanged on 1 August 2026.
  - Left out as an outlier: [Sudstroum's TERRA DYNAMIC](https://www.sudstroum.lu/fr/produits/nouveau-produit-terra-dynamic/), at 0.10000 per kWh plus 30.00 EUR a month, hourly, unchanged since July 2024. With VAT that's 0.10800, if the 0.10000 is without VAT like Sudstroum's other prices.
- **The plan:** lu/client-standard, Creos' client standard. It's for every home with a smart meter (Smarty), which a dynamic contract needs, on any of the four networks, as the fee is national. The fee is 0.05100 without VAT, 0.05508 with VAT, in every hour of every day. So no cheap fee starts at any hour. It matches the [Creos sheet](https://www.creos-net.lu/fileadmin/dokumente/downloads/fr_tarif_electricite_2026.pdf) of 26 November 2025, in the plan's first line, and the 2026 sheets of Enovos, SUDenergie and Sudstroum. Nothing changed during 2026: Enovos' sheet of 1 August 2026 has the same fee. In 2025 it was 0.07590, 0.08197 with VAT ([Creos' 2025 sheet](https://www.creos-net.lu/fileadmin/dokumente/downloads/fr_tarif_electricite_2025.pdf)). The drop came from 150 million euros the state pays toward 2026's network costs (law of 19 December 2025). Only homes with storage heating and a calendar pay less at night, and only on the supplement: 0.00760 instead of 0.07650, from 22:00 to 06:00. For the car's own schedule, 00:00 is where the suppliers' fixed EV hours start. The Encevo group's naturstroum home drive charges 0.09213 without VAT from 00:00 to 06:00, against 0.13213 otherwise. SUDenergie's Mäi Stroum smart, from 1 August 2026, has a winter rate of 0.08440 from 00:00 to 06:00. On SMARD's prices for the year, without VAT, an hour of charging from 18:00 averaged 0.13913. From 00:00 it averaged 0.11002, and from 03:30, the best start, 0.09875.
- **Prices to download:** SMARD's chart_data JSON for series 4169, DE-LU's day-ahead price, in EUR/MWh, per quarter-hour from 1 October 2025. There's a file a week, from Monday 00:00 German time, listed in the [index](https://www.smard.de/app/chart_data/4169/DE/index_quarterhour.json). Example: https://www.smard.de/app/chart_data/4169/DE/4169_DE_quarterhour_1759096800000.json, the week from 29 September 2025. smard_url(4169, ...) and PriceTable::add_smard() in scheduler/market.h build and read the files. All 35,068 quarter-hours from 1 October 2025 00:00 to 1 October 2026 07:00 are there. 15 September 2026 matched Nord Pool's GER area, the board's source for LU, in all 96 quarter-hours. This is the price the dynamic contracts bill: EPEX's DE-LU day-ahead price is the coupled auction's, the same on every exchange.
