# plans/ie/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges.

- **Dynamic contracts:** available since May - June 2026, and rare. The CRU's decision CRU2024121 (26 September 2024) obliges the five suppliers with over 200,000 electricity customers to offer households a Standard Dynamic Price Contract: Bord Gáis Energy, Electric Ireland, Energia, PrePay Power / Yuno Energy and SSE Airtricity. The deadline was 1 October 2025; CRU202517 moved it to 1 June 2026, so none was on offer from October 2025 to mid-May 2026. The bill has a standing charge, a base unit rate (flat, or day, night and peak) and a dynamic unit rate: SEMOpx's half-hourly day-ahead price for ROI-DA, capped at €0.50 per kWh without VAT (the floor of the capacity market's Reliability Option strike price), with no floor, so negative prices pass through. The base unit rate carries the network charges and the supplier's other costs and margin; the PSO levy and VAT come on top. In October 2026 Electric Ireland (base rates from 19 May 2026, sign-up by phone only), Energia (Smart Track, from 2 June 2026), Bord Gáis Energy (Smart Dynamic, June 2026), Yuno Energy and PrePayPower (from June 2026) offer one. SSE Airtricity's site still says its plan isn't available yet. They need a smart meter with a strong signal (CTF 4) and online billing. Neither the CRU nor a supplier has published how many households use one. Nearly all pay a supplier's fixed unit rates: 24 hours, day and night, or smart time of use.
  Sources: https://cruie-live-96ca64acab2247eca8a850a7e54b-5b34f62.divio-media.com/documents/Dynamic_Electricity_Price_Tariffs_-_Decision_Paper_002.pdf, https://cruie-live-96ca64acab2247eca8a850a7e54b-5b34f62.divio-media.com/documents/Notification_to_Suppliers_extending_timeline_for_introduction_of_Standard_Dynamic_Pric.PDF, https://www.bordgaisenergy.ie/day-ahead-market-prices, https://www.sseairtricity.com/ie/home/help-centre/dynamic-tariffs/when-will-the-dynamic-tariff-plan-be-available.
- **VAT:** 9% on household electricity all year, the dynamic unit rate included: Bord Gáis shows the day-ahead prices without VAT and adds it on the bill. The reduced rate was to end on 1 November 2025; Budget 2026 extended it to 31 December 2030 (https://assets.gov.ie/static/documents/15569ee3/Budget_2026_Tax_Policy_Changes_Book.pdf, 2.6), and Budget 2027, in October 2026, left it. Suppliers' prices agree: Bord Gáis's 17.45 c without VAT is 19.02 c with it, and Electric Ireland's July 2026 notice names the 9% rate.
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** nothing, 0.00000 with and without VAT. Electricity Tax doesn't apply to domestic use (https://www.revenue.ie/en/companies-and-charities/excise-and-licences/energy-taxes/electricity-tax/index.aspx). The PSO levy is per month, without VAT: €2.01 from 1 October 2025, €1.46 from 1 December 2025 (CRU2025164), €0.51 from 1 October 2026. It and the standing charge are left out. Capacity, imperfections and market operator charges are the supplier's, inside its rates.
- **Energy price:** with a dynamic contract, the market price with VAT plus the base unit rate by band, which works as the plan. Electric Ireland's, the biggest supplier's, from 19 May 2026 (https://www.electricireland.ie/residential/electricity-and-gas/dynamic-price-plans), with VAT: day 0.19810, night 0.08520, peak 0.22550 (without VAT 0.18170, 0.07820, 0.20690). Its page gives no hours; the standard smart bands are night 23:00 - 08:00, peak 17:00 - 19:00 and day the rest, on local time every day (ESB Networks' statement of charges; Energia says the same). So the cheap rate starts at 23:00:

  ```yaml
  # Prices with VAT: https://www.electricireland.ie/residential/electricity-and-gas/dynamic-price-plans
  name: Electric Ireland dynamic base rates
  currency: EUR
  calendar:
    jan-dec:
      mon-sun: night 08:00 day 17:00 peak 19:00 day 23:00 night
  rates:
    night: 0.08520
    day: 0.19810
    peak: 0.22550
  ```

  Others, with VAT: Energia day 0.21970, night 0.12510, peak 0.22920 from 2 June 2026 (https://www.energia.ie/about-energia/our-tariffs); Bord Gáis one flat rate, 0.16730 until 8 October 2026, then 0.19020 (https://www.bordgaisenergy.ie/home/our-tariffs); Yuno day 0.24790, night 0.11280, peak 0.27460 (a comparison site's figures). The cap never applied: the year's dearest half-hour was €465.77 per MWh, and 59 of 17,568 were negative. The usual EV plan is a fixed one with a cheap night window instead, which the car's own timer reaches. Electric Ireland's Home Electric+ Night Boost from 1 July 2026, with VAT and its 5.5% direct debit and online billing discount: day 08:00 - 23:00 0.37600, night 23:00 - 08:00 0.18540, boost 02:00 - 04:00 0.10880 (https://www.electricireland.ie/residential/electricity-and-gas/ev-night-boost). Its unit rates had stayed the same through the winter and rose 9.5% on 1 July 2026, so they were about 0.34338, 0.16932 and 0.09936 before. Bord Gáis's Urban EV Smart from 9 October 2026, before discounts: EV rate 0.11520, day 0.45220, night 0.34110, peak on weekdays 0.63080. A rough check on the year's prices: the two cheapest half-hours of each night cost 0.19944 per kWh on Electric Ireland's dynamic plan, 0.22658 from 23:00 and 0.41772 at once from 18:00, against 0.10880 at its Night Boost rate. So the example takes Night Boost, at its rates from 1 July 2026 all year.
- **The grid fees by the hour:** households don't pay them themselves: suppliers pay ESB Networks (DUoS) and EirGrid (TUoS) and put them in their unit rates, and in a dynamic contract's base unit rate. The 2025/26 tariffs cover 1 October 2025 - 30 September 2026, the whole year. DUoS for an urban smart home (DG1, time of use), without VAT: day 6.206 c, night 0.802 c, peak 6.827 c per kWh (CRU2025118, Appendix B: https://api.cru.ie/documents/30141/Electricity_Distribution_Network_Allowed_Revenues_and_DUoS_Tariffs_Information_Paper.PDF). TUoS (DTS-D2 at low voltage, with distribution losses), without VAT: day 4.36288 c, night 3.30569 c. That's per MWh a transfer charge of 4.4581 and 4.3927, system services of 29.0912 and 28.6642, and network capacity of 10.0795 by day only, which is 08:00 - 23:00 (EirGrid's Statement of Charges 2025/2026, Appendix 1: https://www.eirgrid.ie/sites/default/files/publications/2025-2026-Statement-of-Charges.pdf). Together, with VAT, for reference only, as they're inside the supplier's rates:

  ```yaml
  # Prices with VAT: https://api.cru.ie/documents/30141/Electricity_Distribution_Network_Allowed_Revenues_and_DUoS_Tariffs_Information_Paper.PDF
  name: DUoS and TUoS, urban smart, 2025/26
  currency: EUR
  calendar:
    jan-dec:
      mon-sun: night 08:00 day 17:00 peak 19:00 day 23:00 night
  rates:
    night: 0.04477
    day: 0.11520
    peak: 0.12197
  ```

  From 1 October 2026 DUoS is day 7.229 c, night 0.934 c, peak 7.952 c (ESB Networks' Statement of Charges, revision 23). Day and night meters without smart services keep night at 23:00 - 08:00 winter time, so 00:00 - 09:00 in summer.
- **Prices to download:** SEMOpx, without an account, in euros per MWh. Its API keeps only the last 365 days (on 11 October 2026 the oldest is delivery day 13 October 2025): the list https://reports.semopx.com/api/v1/documents/static-reports?DPuG_ID=EA-001&ExcludeDelayedPublication=0&ResourceName=MarketResult_SEM-DA_PWR-MRC-D%2B1&Date=%3E2026-09-01%3C2026-10-03&sort_by=Date&order_by=ASC&page_size=100, then each day by its `_id`, like https://reports.semopx.com/api/v1/documents/6a96b3c89620d92e0e7531bb (delivery on 2 September 2026), which add_semopx() reads. Older days are in SEMOpx's yearly archives: https://www.semopx.com/sites/semopx/files/2026-01/DAM%20IDM%20MarketResults%202025.zip (34 MB, all of 2025's auctions) and https://www.semopx.com/sites/semopx/files/2026-09/DAM%20IDM%20MarketResults%202026.zip (31 MB, auctions to 31 August 2026). They hold a CSV a day, like MarketResult_SEM-DA_PWR-MRC-D+1_20250930100000_20250930105930.csv for delivery on 1 October 2025: after the line `Market;ROI-DA` come `Index prices;30;EUR`, the half-hours' starts in UTC and their prices, separated by semicolons, with decimal commas. Checked: archive and API agree, and together hold all 17,568 half-hours from 1 October 2025 to 1 October 2026. The day-ahead market went from hours to half-hours with delivery on 1 October 2025, and a delivery day runs from 23:00 to 23:00 Irish time. Ember's hourly file has Ireland's prices a day late and only each hour's first half: don't use it.
- **Uncertain:** how many households have a dynamic contract, and which plan a typical EV owner is on: nothing is published. Whether SSE Airtricity's delay has consequences. The hours of Electric Ireland's dynamic bands, taken as the standard smart bands with the peak every day (Bord Gáis's own peak is on weekdays only). Yuno's base rates, from a comparison site only. Electric Ireland's Night Boost rates before July 2026, worked out from the 9.5% rise, and the hours of Bord Gáis's EV rate. Energia ties the cap to the strike price floor, which may change weekly; the others say €0.50, and no half-hour reached it.
