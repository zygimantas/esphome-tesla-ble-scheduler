# plans/fi/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges.

- **Dynamic contracts:** common. At the end of 2025, 34% of households had an open-ended spot contract (pörssisähkö), 42% a fixed-term one, a third of them hybrids partly priced by the hour, and 24% an open-ended fixed price (Energiavirasto's press release of 4 May 2026, copied at https://www.epressi.com/tiedotteet/energia/kulutuksen-ajoitus-vaikuttaa-jo-lahes-joka-toisen-kotitalouden-sahkolaskuun.html; spot was 33% at the end of 2024). A spot contract bills Nord Pool's FI day-ahead price with VAT, plus the margin. Since 1 October 2025 that's the quarter-hour's price where the meter measures quarter-hours (Helen's, Vattenfall's, Oomi's and Turku Energia's pages below). An hourly meter's hour is split evenly over its four quarter-hours, which bills the hour's average (https://www.fortum.com/fi/sahkoa/sahkon-hinta/varttisahko, https://oomi.fi/varttisahkon-abc/). About 40% of Caruna's meters measured quarter-hours in September 2025 (https://caruna.fi/ajankohtaista/sahkomarkkinat-siirtyvat-varttihinnoitteluun-lokakuussa-2025), and Caruna replaces all its meters, at about 750,000 sites, in 2027 - 2029 (its news of 10 September 2026). Older contracts moved later, billing the hour's average until then: Fortum's made before October 2025 on 1 December 2025, Oomi's Aktiivinen on 1 April 2026. Homes on a wholly fixed price, about half, gain only from the plan's cheap hours.
- **VAT:** 25.5% since 1 September 2024, on the market price, the margin, the network fees and the electricity tax alike (https://vero.fi/yritykset-ja-yhteisot/verot-ja-maksut/arvonlisaverotus/arvonlisaveroprosentit/). The same from October to December 2025 and all of 2026: only the reduced rate changed, from 14% to 13.5%, in January 2026.
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** only the electricity tax, class I for every home, which the network company bills with its fees (https://caruna.fi/tuotteet-ja-palvelut/kotiin-ja-kiinteistoon/sahkovero). From 1 April 2026 it's the energy tax, 0.02240, and the security of supply fee (huoltovarmuusmaksu), 0.00085: 0.02325 without VAT, 0.02918 with (https://vero.fi/yritykset-ja-yhteisot/verot-ja-maksut/valmisteverotus/sahkovero/verotaulukot/; Caruna's page gives the same, 0.02917875). From October 2025 to March 2026 the fee was 0.00013: 0.02253 without VAT, 0.02828 with (Caruna's news of 20 March 2026). The plans leave the tax out (README.md). Nothing else is billed per kWh: the network bill is a basic fee, the energy fee and the tax (https://caruna.fi/tuotteet-ja-palvelut/kotiin-ja-kiinteistoon/mista-sahkonjakelun-hinta-muodostuu), and the supplier's a basic fee, the spot price and the margin. Left out, as fixed by the month: Caruna Yösiirto's basic fee, 38.03 € with VAT at 3 x 25 A, and the supplier's, about 5 €.
- **Supplier's margin:** 0.00550 with VAT (0.00438 without), the middle of four big national suppliers' spot contracts, checked on 11 October 2026. Each with VAT, without VAT, and the basic fee a month with VAT:
  - Helen Pörssisähkö: 0.00490, 0.00390, 4.96 € (https://www.helen.fi/sahko/porssisahko).
  - Vattenfall Optimi Pörssisähkö: 0.00500, 0.00398, 5.95 € (https://www.vattenfall.fi/sahkosopimukset/porssisahko/).
  - Fortum Tarkka: 0.00590, 0.00470, 5.99 € (https://www.fortum.com/fi/sahkoa/sahkoa-kotiin/tarkka-porssisahko).
  - Oomi Aktiivinen: 0.00600, 0.00478, 4.96 € after four months free (https://oomi.fi/sahko/sahkosopimukset/aktiivinen/).
  - Turku Energia Louna Pörssi, a regional one: 0.00420, 0.00335, 4.95 € (https://www.turkuenergia.fi/kotitaloudet/sahko/sahkosopimukset-ja-hinnat/louna-porssi).

  Energiavirasto's comparison, https://www.sahkonhinta.fi/, lists the same, through its API for a postcode in Caruna's area (https://ev-shv-prod-app-wa-consumerapi1.azurewebsites.net/api/productlist/08100). There the prices date from 28 February 2025 (Fortum), 30 September 2025 (Vattenfall), 2 October 2025 (Helen) and 4 September 2026 (Oomi), so October to December 2025 had the same, Oomi's perhaps excepted. Its household spot offers mostly ask 0.00300 - 0.01000, with a median of 0.00500.
- **The plan:** fi/caruna-yosiirto. Caruna is Finland's largest network company, with 744,000 customers and about a fifth of the country's distribution, 493,000 of them Caruna Oy's (https://caruna.fi/tietoa-meista/caruna-lyhyesti and its page on the price above). No figures say which product EV owners take, so this follows from the arithmetic. A car taking 3,320 kWh a year at night saves about 71 € on Yösiirto's night fee, 0.03125 against Yleissiirto's 0.05258. A house's own use of some 3,500 kWh a year, a third of it at night, makes up the rest of the dearer basic fee, 99.84 € a year more at 3 x 25 A (84.96 € in a block of flats or a row house). Caruna advises Kausisiirto for direct electric heating and heat pumps, whose fee is close at night (0.03225) and the same all day from April to October and on Sundays. Yösiirto's cheap fee runs from 22:00 to 07:00 every day. The plan's prices match Caruna Oy's list, dated 1 September 2024 and still in force on 11 October 2026: night 0.02490 and day 0.04070 without VAT (https://caruna.fi/tuotteet-ja-palvelut/kotiin-ja-kiinteistoon/verkkopalveluhinnastot/verkkopalveluhinnasto-caruna-oy). halpasahko.com showed the same Yleissiirto on 7 May 2026. Caruna Oy didn't change its prices on 1 January 2026, when Caruna Espoo raised its own by 4.4% on average (Kirkkonummen Sanomat, 12 November 2025), and announces no change, so the whole year from October 2025 had these fees.
- **Prices to download:** Elering's API republishes Nord Pool's day-ahead prices for Finland without an account or key, a month a file, as for Lithuania: field data.fi, in EUR/MWh without VAT, by the quarter-hour, with Unix timestamps in UTC. Example: https://dashboard.elering.ee/api/nps/price?start=2025-10-01T00:00:00.000Z&end=2025-11-01T00:00:00.000Z. October 2025 to October 2026 came without a gap. Its monthly averages with VAT equal the FI ones Helen publishes for its spot contract, to 0.0001 € per kWh, for every month from November 2025 to September 2026, and October 2025's within 0.0001 €, as a month in UTC starts three hours into Finland's. So it's the price spot contracts bill. Finland's clock is Lithuania's: UTC+2, with summer time.
- **Uncertain:**
  - Which network product EV owners take: no statistics were found.
  - An hourly meter, as about 60% of Caruna's homes had in September 2025 and keep until the new meters, bills the hour's average, and this car charges for only some 50 minutes a night. On Elering's prices with Yösiirto, the quarter-hours the board picks would then cost about 19 € a year more in market price with VAT (16%) than the example counts. Picking hours by their average instead would cost about 10 € more than it counts. This is my rough calculation, not the example's.
  - Caruna's pages don't say whether its night follows summer time; the plans assume it does.
  - No state support, price cap or EV network product was found for 2025 - 2026; the last support was VAT cut to 10% from December 2022 to April 2023 (vero.fi). The web search budget ran out before a thorough check. Caruna's Yösiirto has no holiday rules, and its household products no power fee.
  - Energiavirasto's own site was behind a bot check, so its contract shares come from the press release's copy on ePressi.
  - Whether VAT applies to a negative price isn't stated anywhere checked. It hardly matters: 236 of the year's 18,980 night quarter-hours were negative, none below -10.01 €/MWh.
