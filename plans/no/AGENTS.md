# plans/no/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges.

- **Dynamic contracts:** common. Spot contracts carried 97.6% of what households bought in the second quarter of 2026, fixed prices 1.0% and variable ones 1.4% ([SSB, table 14491](https://www.ssb.no/statbank/table/14491)). They bill by the hour, not the quarter-hour: homes' meters register hours, and since the market went to quarter-hours a spot contract on such a meter must bill Nord Pool's published hourly price in kroner ([FOR-1999-03-11-301](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/1999-03-11-301) §§ 3-2 and 7-6). That price is the average of the hour's four quarter-hours (Nord Pool's hourly NO1 index for 10 October 2026), as RME recommended ([report 2/2025](https://publikasjoner.nve.no/rme_rapport/2025/rme_rapport2025_02.pdf)), and Fjordkraft's and Fortum's pages say "time for time". So the program should price each quarter-hour at its hour's average. Most of NO1's household electricity is on Norgespris, below, which takes the market price out.
- **State support and Norgespris:** each meter has one or the other ([LOV-2025-06-20-44](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2025-06-20-44) § 8).
  - Strømstøtte, by default: for each hour, the grid bill gives back 90% of the hour's spot price above a threshold, and 25% more for VAT. A kWh's support is 1.125 × max(0, P - T) kroner, with P the hour's spot price without VAT (its quarter-hours' average, not each quarter-hour's) and T 0.77000 in 2026 and 0.75000 from 15 September to 31 December 2025, for the first 5,000 kWh a month per meter ([FOR-2025-09-08-1791](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2025-09-08-1791) § 5; [RME](https://www.nve.no/reguleringsmyndigheten/kunde/stroem/dette-er-stroemstoetteordningen/) writes it as (spot price - 77) × 0.9 × 1.25 øre). The margin gets none. In NO1, 94% of the hours from 22:00 to 07:00 from January to September 2026 were above 0.77000, and 33% of those from October to December 2025 above 0.75000 (Energy-Charts' prices at the ECB's rate), so in 2026 a home paid only a tenth of most nights' price swings.
  - Norgespris, on request: 0.40000 without VAT, 0.50000 with it, from 1 October 2025 to 31 December 2026. For each hour the grid company pays the spot price above it, or charges what's below it, with VAT, for the first 5,000 kWh a month ([FOR-2025-09-08-1790](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2025-09-08-1790) §§ 10, 11 and 13). The meter is then bound to the end of 2026, after 14 days to cancel, and gets no strømstøtte. The supplier's contract, margin and monthly fee stay ([RME](https://www.nve.no/reguleringsmyndigheten/kunde/stroem/dette-er-norgespris/)). So every hour costs 0.50000 plus the margin, and the board can save only the plan's night discount.
  - Norgespris homes used just under 58% of households' electricity at the end of June 2026: 73% in NO1, 82% in NO2 and 26% in NO3, from Elhub's figures ([SSB, 17 August 2026](https://www.ssb.no/energi-og-industri/energi/statistikk/elektrisitetspriser/artikler/lavere-strompris-for-husholdninger)). Elhub counted just over 1.1 million households with it in NO1, NO2 and NO5 on 31 December 2025 ([TU, 26 January 2026](https://www.tu.no/artikler/norgespris-vs-stromstotte-1-0-i-forste-omgang/567402)).
- **VAT:** 25%, on the energy, the margin, the grid fee and the charges below; the support adds it back. None for homes in Nordland, Troms and Finnmark ([merverdiavgiftsloven § 6-6](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2009-06-19-58)), roughly NO4, where the board leaves it out.
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** both billed with the grid fee.
  - The electricity tax (forbruksavgift): 0.07130 without VAT, 0.08913 with it, all of 2026 ([Skatteetaten](https://www.skatteetaten.no/satser/elektrisk-kraft/)). In 2025 it was 0.12530 from October to December, after 0.16930 from April to September ([Skatteetaten, 2025](https://www.skatteetaten.no/satser/elektrisk-kraft/?year=2025)): 0.15663 with VAT. Homes in Finnmark and seven Nord-Troms municipalities pay none.
  - The Enova fee (Energifondet): 0.01000 without VAT, 0.01250 with it, for homes, in 2025 and 2026; businesses pay 800 kr a year instead (Elvia's sheets below; [Midt Nett's 2026 price list](https://midtnett.no/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Prisliste-fra-01.01.2026.pdf)).
  - Their sum with VAT: 0.10163 in 2026, 0.16913 from October to December 2025.
- **Supplier's margin:** 0.05900 with VAT (0.04720 without), the påslag of the big suppliers' standard spot contracts. Prices with VAT and electricity certificates, checked on 10 and 11 October 2026: Fjordkraft Strøm til Spotpris 0.05900 and 69 kr a month ([price list](https://www.fjordkraft.no/strom/stromavtale/prisliste/), updated 10 October 2026), Fortum Spotpris 0.05900 and 49 kr ([price list](https://www.fortum.com/no/strom/stromavtale/prisliste)), Lyse Spotpris 0.05900 and 55 kr ([Lyse](https://www.lyse.no/strom/spotpris)), Motkraft Spot 0.06900 and 59 kr ([Motkraft](https://www.motkraft.no/)) and Tibber 0.03400 and 49 kr ([Tibber](https://tibber.com/no/bytte-stromleverandor)). Forbrukerrådet's price portal had 253 hourly spot contracts in Oslo open to anyone without a membership on 11 October 2026: median 0.05490, monthly fee median 39 kr ([its API](https://strom-api.forbrukerradet.no/products/grid/5)). Hafslund and NorgesEnergi have none there.
- **The plan:** `no/elvia-households`, NO1. Elvia is Norway's largest grid company, in Oslo, Akershus, Østfold and Innlandet ([Elvia](https://www.elvia.no/hva-er-elvia/)). Its night fee runs from 22:00 to 06:00 on workdays and all day at weekends and on public holidays, so its cheap fee starts at 22:00. The plan matches Elvia's [prices from 1 July 2026](https://www.elvia.no/siteassets/dokumenter/priser/2026/1-juli-2026/tariffblad_1_0_standard-tariff_privat_20260701.pdf), 0.46400 and 0.31400 with the tax, the Enova fee and VAT: 0.36238 and 0.21238 without the first two. From 1 October 2025 to 30 June 2026 they were 0.26238 and 0.16238 (sheets of [1 October 2025](https://www.elvia.no/siteassets/dokumenter/priser/2025/1-oktober-2025/tariffblad_1_0_standard_tariff_privat_20251001_elavg_inkl_mva.pdf) and [1 January 2026](https://www.elvia.no/siteassets/dokumenter/priser/2026/tariffblad_1_0_standard-tariff_privat_20260101.pdf)), so for 9 of the 12 months the night discount was 0.10000, not the plan's 0.15000. The plan's moving holidays are 2027's; in 2026 they were 2, 3 and 6 April, 14 May and 25 May.
- **Prices to download:** hvakosterstrommen.no has the hours that are billed, a day a file, free for any use, in kroner at Norges Bank's rate (its EXR field): https://www.hvakosterstrommen.no/api/v1/prices/2025/10-01_NO1.json (24 hours, 23 or 25 when the clocks change). Energy-Charts has NO1's quarter-hours, which the board plans on, in euros per MWh, a month a request: https://api.energy-charts.info/price?bzn=NO1&start=2025-10-01&end=2025-11-01. It had all 35,040 quarter-hours from 1 October 2025 to 30 September 2026, but answers "Too Many Requests" to quick repeats, and its terms allow only private and internal use. Its hourly averages match hvakosterstrommen.no's to 0.01 EUR/MWh, and hvakosterstrommen.no's kroner were about 0.15% under Nord Pool's on 10 October 2026, from Nord Pool's own exchange rate. Nord Pool's API needs an account beyond its last two months.
- **Uncertain:** RME doesn't say how it gets each hour's price for the support and Norgespris from the quarter-hours; the billed average is the likely one. NVE's open tariff data has Elvia at 0.41400 and 0.28900 from 1 July 2026, halfway between the two sheets, and no holidays; the plan follows Elvia's own sheet and page. regjeringen.no is behind a bot check and couldn't be read. The board's scheduler doesn't know the support (total_price() in scheduler/tariff.h): where prices pass the threshold, the plan's night discount matters more than it thinks.
