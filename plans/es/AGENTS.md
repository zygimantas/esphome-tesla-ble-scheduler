# plans/es/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges.

- **Dynamic contracts:** on 31 December 2025 about 8.2 million consumers were on the regulated PVPC (up to 10 kW contracted), 27.4% of household consumers and about 22% of household electricity, and the rest on the free market ([CNMC, page 15](https://www.cnmc.es/sites/default/files/6742152.pdf)). In 2025, 24% of household electricity was billed at a price that changes every hour, 15% at prices that change by period and 61% at one price ([CNMC, page 43](https://www.cnmc.es/sites/default/files/6742152.pdf)), so free-market offers indexed to the hourly price are only a few percent of it, about 4% of the free market's household electricity in the chart on [page 44](https://www.cnmc.es/sites/default/files/6742152.pdf), and the reports give no count of their customers. These offers pass OMIE's price on at cost (Octopus Flexi, niba Flex, Lucera, Gana Energía, Som Energia, Factor Energía), mostly with no margin per kWh but a fee of 3.7 to 5.1 EUR a month; Lucera bills the month's average per 2.0TD period instead. The PVPC's energy cost in 2026 is 45% day-ahead and intraday and 55% futures ([CNMC, page 28](https://www.cnmc.es/sites/default/files/6742152.pdf)), but the futures enter as an adjustment worked out once a day, 3.55 EUR per MWh on average in the first half of 2026, and each hour keeps its own market price in full ([RD 446/2023](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2023-14048)). REE's hourly PVPC from January to September 2026 rises 1.19 EUR per MWh (grid losses included) for each EUR per MWh by which an hour's day-ahead price, the average of its quarter-hours, is above its day's average, and only 0.51 for each EUR of the day's average ([REE](https://apidatos.ree.es/es/datos/mercados/precios-mercados-tiempo-real?start_date=2026-09-01T00:00&end_date=2026-09-30T23:59&time_trunc=hour), a month per request, and [OMIE](https://www.omie.es/es/file-download?parents=marginalpdbc&filename=marginalpdbc_20260930.1) for the quarter-hours), so the difference between a cheap hour and a dear one reaches a PVPC home in full. Household meters record hours, not quarter-hours: practically all have hourly measurement ([CNMC, 18 December 2025, page 4](https://www.cnmc.es/sites/default/files/6340245.pdf)), and the system operator asks for quarter-hours only from the larger point types ([BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2025-6693)), although the day-ahead market has priced every quarter-hour since 1 October 2025 ([OMIE](https://www.omie.es/sites/default/files/2025-10/2501001_go_live_mtu15_md_es_vf.pdf)). So the PVPC bills each hour at the average of its four quarter-hour prices, which REE's series follows rather than the hour's first quarter, and so does Gana Energía's indexed offer ([Gana Energía, 2 October 2025](https://ganaenergia.com/blog/nuevo-mercado-cuarto-horario-de-luz-2025-como-afecta-a-mi-factura/)): the board's choice of quarter-hours within an hour changes nothing on the bill.
- **VAT:** 21%, but 10% for contracts under 10 kW from 22 March to 31 May 2026 (RDL 7/2026, ended early by RDL 18/2026).
- **Electricity tax (IEE):** 5.11269632% of the supply before VAT, VAT then on top (Ley 38/1992, art. 99), a multiplier of 1.0511269632 on everything else. From 22 March to 31 May 2026 it was 0.5%, with a minimum of 1 EUR per MWh. The program uses the normal rates all year.
- **Per kWh, besides the market price:** what indexed offers add, before VAT: grid losses, about 15% of the market price at night (REE sets them by the hour; Octopus uses 14.4% in the valley period); the municipal rate, 1.5% of the energy part; balancing services, 20.8 EUR per MWh on average from October 2025 to September 2026 ([REE's price components](https://apidatos.ree.es/es/datos/mercados/componentes-precio-energia-cierre-desglose?start_date=2025-10-01T00:00&end_date=2026-09-30T23:59&time_trunc=month)), about 0.024 EUR at the meter; the energy savings fund (FNEE), 0.0024 EUR from 28 February 2026; the system and market operators' fees, 0.00024 EUR; imbalances, about 0.0005 EUR. Capacity payments are 0 in the valley period. Together, about 0.027 EUR plus 16.7% of the market price, before VAT: 0.040 EUR at the period's average price, 0.048 with VAT.
- **Supplier's margin:** none in the offers above; Som Energia adds 0.020 EUR (0.0242 with VAT) and Factor Energía 0.0102 EUR.
- **The plan:** `es/2-0td`, the access toll every household up to 15 kW pays, whose valley period starts at 00:00 on weekdays and lasts all weekend and on the fixed national holidays. Its prices are 2026's tolls ([CNMC](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2025-26348)) and charges ([Orden TED/1524/2025](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2025-26705)) with 21% VAT, unchanged from January to September 2026; 2025's were 0.11197, 0.03412 and 0.00362 EUR with VAT.
- **Fixed, left out:** the power term, meter rental (0.81 EUR a month) and the bono social financing (0.019 to 0.025 EUR a day).
- **Prices to download:** OMIE's daily files, as the board downloads them, like https://www.omie.es/es/file-download?parents=marginalpdbc&filename=marginalpdbc_20260101.1. OMIE keeps only a day's latest file, numbered .2 or .3 when it corrects one: 30 October 2025's is .3.
- **Uncertain:** grid losses (no regulated table for 2.0TD), and how each supplier passes balancing costs on. The number of free-market customers on hourly indexed offers is unknown: the CNMC reports energy, not customers, and its figures imply about 2 to 3% of household electricity (24% at hourly prices in 2025 less the PVPC's 22%, or 4% of the free market's in its chart). The 8.2 million PVPC consumers are supply points, not households (30.7 million supply points in all against 19.7 million households, [INE](https://ine.es/dyngs/Prensa/ECP3T25.pdf)), so no share of households follows. Not found: a date for quarter-hour meters in homes, whether indexed offers other than Gana Energía's average an hour's quarter-hours, and whether the CNMC's suggestion in its July 2025 report to drop the PVPC's futures adjustment ([CNMC, page 56](https://www.cnmc.es/sites/default/files/6099794.pdf)) goes ahead.
