# plans/at/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges.

- **Dynamic contracts:** Households mostly pay one price at every hour. In April 2025 about 90% of household products were fixed-price products, a little over 6% monthly floaters, over 3% time-of-use products and 1% spot products (E-Control's Tätigkeitsbericht 2026, pages 61 - 63: https://www.e-control.at/documents/1785851/1811582/E-Control-Taetigkeitsbericht-2026-Barrierefrei.pdf/537a1fa1-a945-2ee5-55bc-68ff56da5c36). It counted about 18 spot products at the end of 2025. Since 1 April 2026 every supplier with more than 25,000 metering points must offer one (ElWG § 22, BGBl. I Nr. 91/2025: https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/I/2025/91). They need a smart meter that sends quarter-hours to the supplier: about 472,000 did at the end of 2024, 119,000 of them in energy communities (E-Control's Smart Meter report 2025, page 26). Nearly all bill "EPEX Spot AT", the day-ahead price of the Austrian bidding zone from the coupled auction (SDAC), the same price Nord Pool and SMARD publish. Most bill it by the hour, as the average of the hour's four quarter-hours: aWATTar, smartENERGY, Energie Steiermark and Wien Energie. Since the market went to quarter-hours on 1 October 2025, VKW and Energie AG bill quarter-hours, and EVN's FAQ says its spot tariff does too. Salzburg AG bills the hourly price of EXAA's own auction instead. Tibber's site lists no Austrian offer.
- **VAT:** 20% on everything, the levies included, from October 2025 to September 2026. Electricity has no reduced rate (https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/steuern-finanzen/umsatzsteuer-ueberblick/steuersaetze-und-steuerbefreiungen-der-umsatzsteuer.html, and every price sheet below).
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** In 2026, for a household (network level 7 without power metering):
  - Electricity tax (Elektrizitätsabgabe): 0.00100 EUR, 0.00120 with VAT, from 1 January to 31 December 2026, for natural persons on a household load profile (H0, HA or HF); other metering points pay 0.00820 (§ 7 Abs. 16 Elektrizitätsabgabegesetz, BGBl. I Nr. 95/2025: https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/I/2025/95, and motion 658/A). Until 31 December 2025 it was 0.01500, 0.01800 with VAT.
  - Renewables levy (Erneuerbaren-Förderbeitrag): 0.00583 tied to the usage fee and 0.00037 tied to the loss fee, 0.00620 EUR, 0.00744 with VAT (Erneuerbaren-Förderbeitragsverordnung 2026, BGBl. II Nr. 301/2025: https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/II/2025/301). In 2025 it was 0.00796, 0.00955 with VAT (BGBl. II Nr. 419/2024).
  - Their sum with VAT: 0.00864 EUR. In October - December 2025 it was 0.02755, 0.01891 more at every hour. Wiener Netze's sheets give the same: https://www.wienernetze.at/o/document/wn_ex0106_steuernundabgaben_202603_bf and https://www.wienernetze.at/o/document/ex104_erneuerbaren-forderpauschale-und-forderbeitrag_2026_bf-pdf.
  - Fixed, left out: the levy's 3.796 EUR a year per metering point, the Erneuerbaren-Förderpauschale (19.02 EUR a year, 2025 - 2027), the network's 54.00 EUR a year (48.00 in 2025) and the meter fee.
  - A share of the bill: Vienna's Gebrauchsabgabe, a municipal levy, is 7% of the net energy price and the net network price (usage, losses, meter) since 1 March 2026, 6% before. It isn't charged on the electricity tax or the renewables levies, and VAT comes on top (Wiener Netze's sheet above; Wien Energie's price sheet of 1 October 2026: https://www.wienenergie.at/preisblatt-optimaentspanntplus). So in Vienna the multiplier is 1.07 on the market price, the margin and the plan's fee. Applied to the flat part too, it needs 0.00807 there instead of 0.00864. Wiener Netze's parts of Lower Austria and Burgenland don't pay it.
- **Supplier's margin:** 0.01800 EUR with VAT (0.01500 without), aWATTar's, about the middle of seven offers. All were checked on 11 October 2026, per kWh without VAT, with their monthly fee without VAT:
  - smartENERGY smartCONTROL: hourly price + 0.01200 (0.01440 with VAT), 2.49 EUR, sheet valid from 1 February 2024: https://www.smartenergy.at/smartcontrol
  - Energie Steiermark SteirerStrom Spot, the same company: hourly + 0.01200, 3.18 EUR, valid from 1 June 2026: https://www.e-steiermark.com/privat/produkte/strom/spottarif
  - VKW Strom Dynamisch, Vorarlberg only: quarter-hours + 0.01200, 3.00 EUR: https://www.vkw.at/produkte/strom/strom-dynamisch
  - Wien Energie OPTIMA Voll Aktiv, for its existing customers only: hourly + 0.01420 + 7% of the market price, about 4.32 EUR: https://www.wienenergie.at/privat/produkte/strom/optima-voll-aktiv/
  - aWATTar HOURLY: hourly + 0.01500 (0.01800), 4.79 EUR, sheet valid for 11 October 2026: https://www.awattar.at/tariffs/hourly
  - Salzburg AG Strom FlexSpot: EXAA's hourly price + 3% + 0.01800, 4.17 EUR, capped at 0.76320 with VAT: https://www.salzburg-ag.at/strom/privat/stromprodukte/strom-flexspot.html
  - Energie AG Ökostrom SPOT: quarter-hours + 0.02500 (0.01667 in the first year with its bonus), 4.50 EUR: https://www.energieag.at/privat/strom/flexible-tarife/spot
- **The plan:** at/wiener-netze. Wiener Netze calls itself Austria's largest network operator, with 1.6 million electricity meters (https://www.wienernetze.at/wir-in-zahlen). E-Control counted 1.64 million of Austria's 6.36 million metering points in the Vienna area at the end of 2024, the most of any area (Statistikbroschüre 2025). Its fee is the same at every hour of the night all year: the only lower fee is SNAP, from 10:00 to 16:00, April to September. So no cheap fee starts at night. On the market alone, a 50-minute charge cost least from about 03:30 over the 365 nights (0.11330 against 0.15420 EUR from 18:00, without VAT, from SMARD's prices). Its prices, 0.06980 for usage (0.05580 SNAP) and 0.00700 for losses without VAT, make 0.09216 and 0.07536 with VAT, as in the plan. They match the SNE-V 2018 - Novelle 2026 (BGBl. II Nr. 305/2025), the plan's first line, and Wiener Netze's own sheet, valid since 1 January 2026 and still current (https://www.wienernetze.at/o/document/wn_ex0105-netznutzungs-und-netzverlustentgelt_2026_v2_01_bf). All 13 plans in this folder match the SNE-V. In October - December 2025 the fee was 0.07400 + 0.00700, 0.09720 with VAT, 0.00504 more at every hour (BGBl. II Nr. 370/2024).
- **Prices to download:** SMARD's series 4170, Austria's day-ahead price in EUR per MWh, a file a week from Monday 00:00 German time, without an account or key. Example: https://www.smard.de/app/chart_data/4170/DE/4170_DE_quarterhour_1766962800000.json, with the weeks listed in https://www.smard.de/app/chart_data/4170/DE/index_quarterhour.json. It has distinct quarter-hours from 1 October 2025 and every quarter-hour to 1 October 2026 07:00. It matched Nord Pool's AT prices, the board's, on all 96 quarter-hours of 6 October 2026. Its hourly averages matched aWATTar's billed hourly prices on 12 October 2025 (https://api.awattar.at/v1/marketdata?start=1760220000000&end=1760306400000). So it's the price the EPEX-based contracts bill, by quarter-hour or as the hour's average, but not Salzburg AG's EXAA price.
- **Uncertain:**
  - Today's share of dynamic contracts: E-Control's 1% is from April 2025, before ElWG § 22 and quarter-hour prices. Its yearly report under ElWG § 22 (6) wasn't found.
  - Most dynamic contracts bill hourly averages, so a schedule by quarter-hours saves them a little less than the model shows.
  - EV owners: Vienna had 45,628 of Austria's 257,717 battery cars at the end of 2025, Lower Austria 52,105 and Upper Austria 51,343 (Statistik Austria). Home chargers are probably more often in houses outside Vienna, without the Gebrauchsabgabe. Every plan here is flat at night, so the plan changes the totals, not the saving.
  - A wallbox on a meter of its own, like an interruptible one (Vienna: 0.04210 + 0.00700 without VAT, no yearly fee), may not have a household load profile and then pays 0.00820 electricity tax.
  - Gebrauchsabgaben elsewhere: aWATTar's sheet says up to 6% in some municipalities. Vienna's law itself wasn't read; its 7% is from Wiener Netze's and Wien Energie's sheets.
  - Whether Tibber still supplies Austrian homes.
  - The electricity tax goes back to 0.01500 on 1 January 2027 unless extended, and E-Control's draft for 2027 adds a lower winter fee from 22:00 to 04:00.
