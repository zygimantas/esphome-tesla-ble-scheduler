# plans/dk/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges.

- **Dynamic contracts:** Common. Most homes pay a variable price: the day-ahead price of their area, DK1 west of the Great Belt or DK2 east of it, plus the supplier's margin. Suppliers with over 200,000 customers must offer one, and it must follow the spot market in steps no longer than the market's ([BEK nr. 1810 af 29. december 2025](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2025/1810), § 2 and § 26). No official share for homes was found: ACER's country sheet says Denmark doesn't monitor it. Among small business customers, under 20 MWh a year, 83% had a variable price in the second half of 2025 ([Energistyrelsen](https://ens.dk/media/8491/download)). The market went to quarter-hours on 1 October 2025, but the bill follows the readings the grid company sends: hourly readings pay the average of the hour's four quarter-hour prices, quarter-hour readings each quarter-hour's own ([Andel Energi](https://andelenergi.dk/el/flexenergi/), [Forbrugerrådet Tænk, 29 September 2025](https://taenk.dk/forbrugerliv/bolig/flere-elpriser-i-doegnet-giver-minimale-besparelser)). Radius's meters read by the hour unless the customer asks the supplier for quarter-hours, which can't be undone ([Radius](https://radiuselnet.dk/din-tilslutning/elmaaler/)).
- **VAT:** 25% on the whole bill: the market price, the margin, the grid fees and the tax. There's no lower rate for electricity. Skatteministeriet counts its tax cut of 71.9 øre on 4,000 kWh as about 3,600 kr with VAT, EWII gives the tax as 90 øre with VAT in 2025 and 1 øre in 2026 (72 and 0.8 øre without), and Radius's price page multiplies DataHub's prices by 1.25.
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** in 2026, without and with VAT:
  - Electricity tax (elafgift): 0.00800 and 0.01000, the EU minimum, from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2027 ([Skattestyrelsen](https://skat.dk/erhverv/afgifter-paa-varer-og-ydelser-punktafgifter/nyhedsbrev-afgifter/midlertidig-nedsaettelse-af-elafgiften-i-2026-og-2027), [Skatteministeriet's fact sheet, August 2025](https://svmn.dk/media/fg2pvujk/faktaark-om-lempelse-af-elafgift.pdf), DataHub's EA-001).
  - Energinet's net tariff (nettarif, for the distribution grid): 0.04300 and 0.05375.
  - Energinet's system tariff (systemtarif): 0.07200 and 0.09000. Both hold all of 2026: [Energinet's tariff catalogue for 2026](https://www.energinet.dk/media/ikylsqle/energinets-tarifkatalog-2026.pdf), page 7, and DataHub's 40000 and 41000 (`https://api.energidataservice.dk/dataset/DatahubPricelist?filter={"ChargeTypeCode":["40000","41000","EA-001"]}&start=2025-01-01`).
  - Sum: 0.12300 without VAT, 0.15375 with. The plans have none of these, only the grid company's own fee.
  - October to December 2025 had tax 0.72000, net tariff 0.06100 and system tariff 0.07400 without VAT: 1.06875 with VAT, 0.91500 more per kWh than in 2026. As they are the same every hour, they change the costs and the percentages, not the saving in kroner.
  - Until 2025, a home charger run by a registered charging operator, like Clever's, got most of the tax back. In 2026 that refund is 0.4 øre ([Clever's 0.004 kr for September 2026](https://clever.dk/satser-tillaeg-og-priser/tilbagebetaling/)), so it's left out. No price cap, state support or grid tariff for cars was found for 2025 or 2026.
  - Left out as fixed: Energinet's system subscription, 187 kr a year per meter, Radius's subscription, 441 kr a year without VAT and 490 kr from April 2026, and the supplier's monthly fee.
- **Supplier's margin:** 0.10000 with VAT (0.08000 without), about the middle of the big suppliers' spot offers, read on 11 October 2026:
  - Andel Energi FlexEnergi: 0.14630 with VAT, for new customers, and 20 kr a month, reset on 1 January and 1 July ([page](https://andelenergi.dk/el/flexenergi/)).
  - Norlys FlexEl: 0.09700 (0.00700 trading costs and 0.09000 administration) from 1 July to 31 December 2026, and 29 kr a month, by the hour ([page](https://norlys.dk/el/flexel/)).
  - Modstrøm EnkEL: 0.10000 and 10 kr a month ([page](https://modstroem.dk/)).
  - EWII Plus El: 0.06500 and 29 kr a month, by quarter-hour where the meter allows ([page](https://www.ewii.dk/privat/el/plus-el/)).
  - OK El: 0.00000 and 49 kr a month above 3,100 kWh a year, or 0.19000 and no monthly fee below ([page](https://www.ok.dk/privat/produkter/el/bestil-ok-el/ok-el-hoejt-forbrug)).
  - Prices for homes are given with VAT in Denmark, though only Andel's and OK's pages say so. Small suppliers go lower, like Vindstød's Go'Vind at 0.01000 ([page](https://vindstoed.dk/elaftaler)).
- **The plan:** dk/radius-nettarif-c, in DK2. Radius Elnet is the biggest grid company, with about 1,100,000 homes and businesses around Copenhagen and in North and Central Zealand ([Radius](https://radiuselnet.dk/priser/)). N1 in Jutland, DK1 (dk/n1-nettarif-c), comes next with over 800,000 ([N1](https://n1.dk/om-n1)). The low fee runs 00:00 - 06:00 every day, so the car's own schedule would start at 00:00. Summer is April - September, winter October - March. There are no weekend or holiday rules: DataHub holds the C tariff as one day's profile per season, while tariffs that change at weekends, like Radius's A høj, get separate weekday and weekend records. Radius changed its fees on 1 April 2026, not on 1 January. DataHub's DT_C_01, which Radius's price page shows ([its data](https://cerius-radius-web-server-prod.azurewebsites.net/api/datahubprice?grid=radius&validYear=2026)), low, high and peak without VAT:
  - 1 October 2025 - 31 March 2026: 0.09760, 0.29290, 0.87880 (with VAT 0.12200, 0.36613, 1.09850), unchanged since December 2024.
  - 1 April - 30 September 2026: 0.10618, 0.15926, 0.41408 (with VAT 0.13272, 0.19908, 0.51760): the plan's summer.
  - From 1 October 2026: 0.10618, 0.31852, 0.95557 (with VAT 0.13272, 0.39816, 1.19447): the plan's winter.
  - So the plan's summer is 2026's, but its winter is the one from 1 October 2026, 8.8% dearer than the winter in the example. Radius's overview "pr. 1. januar 2026" ([historical prices](https://radiuselnet.dk/historiske-priser/)) gives C 9.71, 29.12 and 87.36 øre in winter and 9.71, 14.56 and 37.86 øre in summer, which DataHub never had.
- **Prices to download:** Energi Data Service's DayAheadPrices, without an account or key: quarter-hours from 1 October 2025 (the hours before are in Elspotprices), in DKK and EUR per MWh, with start and end in Danish time: `https://api.energidataservice.dk/dataset/DayAheadPrices?start=2025-10-01T00:00&end=2025-10-02T00:00&filter={"PriceArea":["DK2"]}&sort=TimeUTC%20asc`. It has all 35,040 quarter-hours of the year for each area, and answers 429 to quick bursts. Its DKK is the EUR price at Danmarks Nationalbank's rate. SMARD has the same EUR prices, series 252 for DK1 and 253 for DK2 (`https://www.smard.de/app/chart_data/253/DE/253_DE_quarterhour_<ms>.json`, the weeks listed in `index_quarterhour.json`): both give 102.44, 92.10, 85.90 and 85.27 EUR/MWh for DK2's first quarter-hours of 1 October 2025. These are the prices variable contracts bill, as the hour's average where the meter reads by the hour.
- **Uncertain:** the share of homes with a variable price; whether Norlys's, Modstrøm's and EWII's margins include VAT, as their pages don't say; why Radius's overview for January 2026 differs from DataHub; how many Radius homes have quarter-hour readings; the margins in October to December 2025, as Andel and Norlys change theirs on 1 January and 1 July.
