# plans/de/

Germany's research, checked in October 2026, for this folder's plans and the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp). Start an update here, re-checking the sources under References and the dates under Changes ahead, rather than researching afresh.

## Summary

On a dynamic tariff with a smart meter, the board's charging cost 746.84 EUR over the year from 1 October 2025: 50% less than charging at once (1,487.64 EUR), 8% less than the car's own schedule from 00:00 (807.73 EUR) and 1% less than its best start, 03:30 (757.16 EUR). Confidence in the README is Low: dynamic contracts and Modul 3 need a smart meter, and 6% of meters are one.

## Decisions

- **Contract:** a dynamic tariff (§ 41a EnWG) with a smart meter, billing the DE-LU price of each quarter-hour: the price the board downloads.
- **Plan:** `de/westnetz-modul-3`, as Westnetz is the largest grid operator. The car's own schedule starts at 00:00, when Modul 3's low fee does.
- **Billing:** each quarter-hour at its own price.
- **Margin:** 0.020 EUR per kWh with VAT, about the middle of four offers.
- **The same every hour:** 0.07837 EUR per kWh with VAT: the electricity tax, the concession fee and the levies.
- **Left out:** monthly fees, as they don't depend on when the car charges.
- **Market prices:** SMARD's series 4169.

## Findings

- **Who pays the market price:** few homes. The Monitoringbericht gives no count of dynamic contracts, only of the suppliers that offered one: 412 in 2024 and 90 in 2023 ([Bundesnetzagentur and Bundeskartellamt, 26 November 2025](https://data.bundesnetzagentur.de/Bundesnetzagentur/SharedDocs/Mediathek/Monitoringberichte/MonitoringberichtEnergie2025.pdf)). ACER's report on the regulators' 2024 data puts German households in its lowest band for dynamic contracts, 0 to 5%, with 99% of household contracts at a flat price ([ACER and CEER, November 2025](https://www.acer.europa.eu/sites/default/files/documents/Publications/ACER-CEER-2025-Retail-monitoring.pdf)). A forsa survey in July 2024 had 7% of households say they use a dynamic tariff ([vzbv, October 2024](https://www.vzbv.de/sites/default/files/2024-10/Dynamische%20Stromtarife_repr%C3%A4sentative%20Befragung%202024.pdf)), more than the 1.1 million smart meters in homes and small businesses at the end of 2024 ([Bundesnetzagentur and Bundeskartellamt, table 48](https://data.bundesnetzagentur.de/Bundesnetzagentur/SharedDocs/Mediathek/Monitoringberichte/MonitoringberichtEnergie2025.pdf)), so it must include contracts billed without one.
- **Meters and billing:** only a home with a smart meter (intelligentes Messsystem, iMSys) pays the price of the hour or quarter-hour it used. Suppliers have to offer the contract to homes that have one ([§ 41a EnWG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/enwg_2005/__41a.html)). Without one the price is a monthly average, a typical load curve or a fixed price, so charging at night does not lower the bill ([Rabot](https://www.rabot.energy/fragen-antworten/), [Octopus](https://octopusenergy.de/dynamic-octopus), [Finanztip](https://www.finanztip.de/stromtarife/dynamischer-stromtarif/)). Tibber, Rabot, Octopus and naturstrom bill the 15-minute price to homes with an iMSys ([Tibber](https://tibber.com/de/stromtarif), [Rabot](https://www.rabot.energy/fragen-antworten/), [Octopus](https://octopusenergy.de/dynamic-octopus), [naturstrom](https://www.naturstrom.de/privatkunden/oekostrom/naturstrom-smart)); tado's tariff pages still say hourly ([tado](https://energy.tado.com/tariffs/hourly)), and Finanztip says some suppliers still bill hourly prices ([Finanztip](https://www.finanztip.de/stromtarife/dynamischer-stromtarif/)). A supplier switches to quarter-hour billing several weeks to about three months after the iMSys is fitted ([Octopus](https://octopusenergy.de/dynamic-octopus), [Rabot](https://www.rabot.energy/fragen-antworten/), [naturstrom](https://www.naturstrom.de/privatkunden/oekostrom/naturstrom-smart)), and the Bundesnetzagentur says a fitted iMSys must also deliver quarter-hour values reliably ([Bundesnetzagentur, 21 September 2026](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/DE/2026/20260921_Rollout.html)).
- **Smart meters:** the Bundesnetzagentur counted 3,172,579 iMSys on 30 June 2026, 5.9% of all 54,135,202 metering locations, businesses included, against 4.4% on 31 December 2025 ([Bundesnetzagentur](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Fachthemen/ElektrizitaetundGas/NetzzugangMesswesen/Mess-undZaehlwesen/iMSys/artikel.html)). A home with a wallbox over 4.2 kW under § 14a is a mandatory iMSys case ([§ 29 MsbG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/messbg/__29.html)), but its meter operator has to meet quotas, not a date for each home: the first was 20% of all mandatory cases by the end of 2025 ([§ 45 MsbG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/messbg/__45.html)), and the later ones are under Changes ahead. On 30 June 2026, 26.1% of the mandatory cases had one, 1,259,270 of 4,831,178 ([Bundesnetzagentur](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Fachthemen/ElektrizitaetundGas/NetzzugangMesswesen/Mess-undZaehlwesen/iMSys/artikel.html)). Any home can also order one, due within four months unless the operator postpones it, for a one-off fee of up to 100 EUR ([§ 34 MsbG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/messbg/__34.html), [§ 35 MsbG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/messbg/__35.html)).
- **VAT:** 19%.
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** 6.586 ct without VAT, 7.837 ct with it, unchanged from January to September 2026. Without VAT:
  - the electricity tax, 2.050 ct ([§ 3 StromStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stromstg/__3.html)); the cut to the EU minimum is for industry and farming only;
  - the concession fee, 1.59 ct for a town of up to 100,000, as for the median home in Westnetz's area; by the town's size it's 1.32, 1.59, 1.99 or 2.39 ct ([§ 2 KAV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kav/__2.html)). Dynamic contracts pay the full rate, not the 0.61 ct for off-peak supply;
  - the KWKG levy, 0.446 ct, the offshore network levy, 0.941 ct, and the § 19 StromNEV levy, 1.559 ct for the first GWh ([netztransparenz.de](https://www.netztransparenz.de/de-de/Erneuerbare-Energien-und-Umlagen)). In 2025 they were 0.277, 0.816 and 1.558 ct.
- **Margins:** naturstrom smart 1.19 ct, tado Hourly 1.785 ct, Tibber 2.15 ct and badenova Ökostrom Vario 3.00 ct per kWh, all with VAT, with monthly fees of 4.58 to 8.50 EUR (October 2026). The example's 2.0 ct with VAT, 1.68 ct without, is about their middle.
- **The plan:** `de/westnetz-modul-3`'s prices match Westnetz's 2026 sheet: 0.95 ct low, from 00:00 to 07:00, 9.53 ct standard and 15.65 ct high, without VAT. Modul 3, due from grid operators since 1 April 2025 ([Bundesnetzagentur, 28 May 2026](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/DE/2026/20260528_14a.html)), is for a home with a controllable device over 4.2 kW, such as a wallbox put into operation since 1 January 2024 or an older one moved into the rules ([Bundesnetzagentur](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Vportal/Energie/SteuerbareVBE/start.html)), that chose it on top of Modul 1 and has an iMSys and no registered load measurement. It prices all grid power billed under that market location (Marktlokation), and the operator may bill the standard price in up to two quarters of the year ([Bundesnetzagentur, 17 July 2026](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Beschlusskammern/BK08/BK8_06_Netzentgelte/68_Para14a_EnWG/FAQ/start.html)).
- **Who has Modul 3:** fewer homes than pay quarter-hour prices. At the end of 2024, 264,874 market locations had controllable devices under the new § 14a rules, among them 62,104 charge points on Modul 1 and 2,488 on Modul 2 ([Bundesnetzagentur and Bundeskartellamt](https://data.bundesnetzagentur.de/Bundesnetzagentur/SharedDocs/Mediathek/Monitoringberichte/MonitoringberichtEnergie2025.pdf)). A study based on two dynamic-tariff suppliers' experience found 14 of 169 grid operators, Westnetz among them, letting customers switch to Modul 3 in May 2026 ([ZFK, 29 May 2026](https://www.zfk.de/energie/erneuerbare/zeitvariable-netzentgelte-umsetzung-netzbetreiber-modul-3)), and a Finanztip survey of 20 large suppliers found 7 letting customers use it, which EnBW disputes for itself ([pv magazine, 23 September 2026](https://www.pv-magazine.de/2026/09/23/viele-versorger-geben-netzentgelt-rabatte-nur-eingeschraenkt-an-die-kunden-weiter/)). When checked in October 2026, the Bundesnetzagentur's list of enforcement showed only fine threats against two grid operators, with a deadline of 30 September 2026 ([Bundesnetzagentur](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/1107472)).
- **Market prices:** SMARD's series 4169, a week a file, like https://www.smard.de/app/chart_data/4169/DE/4169_DE_quarterhour_1766962800000.json. It matched Nord Pool's GER prices, which the board downloads, on all 4,992 quarter-hours from 10 August to 30 September 2026.

## Changes ahead

- **Each year:** each operator's Preisblatt for the next year, preliminary by 15 October and final for 1 January, with Modul 3's hours, which can also change by quarter; the levies each 1 January.
- **End of 2026 and 2032:** the next smart meter quotas: 90% of the mandatory cases that arise from 25 February 2025 to the end of 2026, by the end of that period, and 90% of all by the end of 2032 ([§ 45 MsbG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/messbg/__45.html)).
- **From 2027:** the Bundesnetzagentur wants to open the modules further, if possible ([ZFK, 24 June 2026](https://www.zfk.de/politik/regulierung/zeitvariable-netzentgelte-bundesnetzagentur-haller-neuer-kurs)).

## Open questions

- **How many homes pay each quarter-hour's price, or have Modul 3:** not known. The Monitoringbericht 2026 isn't out; its data was collected from 16 March to 30 April 2026 ([Bundesnetzagentur](https://bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Fachthemen/ElektrizitaetundGas/Monitoringberichte/Datenerhebung/artikel.html)).
- **Smart meters in homes:** the 5.9% counts businesses too, and the Bundesnetzagentur expects it to be low, as not every competitive meter operator reports ([Bundesnetzagentur](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Fachthemen/ElektrizitaetundGas/NetzzugangMesswesen/Mess-undZaehlwesen/iMSys/artikel.html)).
- **tado's billing:** its pages don't say whether it bills quarter-hours.
- **Margins:** they come from four offers only, and Tibber doesn't say whether its 2.15 ct includes VAT.
