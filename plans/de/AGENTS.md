# plans/de/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges.

- **Dynamic contracts:** few homes, and the Monitoringbericht gives no count of their contracts, only of suppliers: 412 offered a dynamic tariff in 2024 and 90 in 2023 ([Bundesnetzagentur and Bundeskartellamt, 26 November 2025](https://data.bundesnetzagentur.de/Bundesnetzagentur/SharedDocs/Mediathek/Monitoringberichte/MonitoringberichtEnergie2025.pdf)). ACER's report on the regulators' 2024 data puts German households in its lowest band for dynamic contracts, 0 to 5%, with 99% of household contracts at a flat price ([ACER and CEER, November 2025](https://www.acer.europa.eu/sites/default/files/documents/Publications/ACER-CEER-2025-Retail-monitoring.pdf)). Only a home with a smart meter (intelligentes Messsystem, iMSys) pays the price of the hour or quarter-hour it used: suppliers have to offer the contract to homes that have one ([§ 41a EnWG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/enwg_2005/__41a.html)), and without one the price is a monthly average, a typical load curve or a fixed price, so charging at night does not lower the bill ([Rabot](https://www.rabot.energy/fragen-antworten/), [Octopus](https://octopusenergy.de/dynamic-octopus), [Finanztip](https://www.finanztip.de/stromtarife/dynamischer-stromtarif/)). The Bundesnetzagentur counted 3,172,579 iMSys on 30 June 2026, 5.9% of all 54,135,202 metering locations, businesses included, against 4.4% on 31 December 2025 ([Bundesnetzagentur](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Fachthemen/ElektrizitaetundGas/NetzzugangMesswesen/Mess-undZaehlwesen/iMSys/artikel.html)). Tibber, Rabot, Octopus and naturstrom bill the 15-minute price to homes with an iMSys ([Tibber](https://tibber.com/de/stromtarif), [Rabot](https://www.rabot.energy/fragen-antworten/), [Octopus](https://octopusenergy.de/dynamic-octopus), [naturstrom](https://www.naturstrom.de/privatkunden/oekostrom/naturstrom-smart)); tado's tariff pages still say hourly ([tado](https://energy.tado.com/tariffs/hourly)), and Finanztip says some suppliers still bill hourly prices ([Finanztip](https://www.finanztip.de/stromtarife/dynamischer-stromtarif/)). A home with a wallbox over 4.2 kW under § 14a is a mandatory iMSys case ([§ 29 MsbG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/messbg/__29.html)), but its meter operator has to meet quotas, not a date for each home: 20% of all mandatory cases by the end of 2025, 90% of the cases that arise from 25 February 2025 to the end of 2026 by the end of that period, and 90% of all by the end of 2032 ([§ 45 MsbG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/messbg/__45.html)); 26.1% (1,259,270 of 4,831,178) had one on 30 June 2026 ([Bundesnetzagentur](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Fachthemen/ElektrizitaetundGas/NetzzugangMesswesen/Mess-undZaehlwesen/iMSys/artikel.html)). Any home can also order one, due within four months unless the operator postpones it, for a one-off fee of up to 100 EUR ([§ 34 MsbG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/messbg/__34.html), [§ 35 MsbG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/messbg/__35.html)).
- **VAT:** 19%.
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** 6.586 ct without VAT, 7.837 ct with it, unchanged from January to September 2026:
  - the electricity tax, 2.050 ct ([§ 3 StromStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stromstg/__3.html)); the cut to the EU minimum is for industry and farming only;
  - the concession fee, 1.59 ct for a town of up to 100,000 ([§ 2 KAV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kav/__2.html)): the median home in Westnetz's area, where it's 1.32, 1.59, 1.99 or 2.39 ct by the town's size. Dynamic contracts pay the full rate, not the 0.61 ct for off-peak supply;
  - the KWKG levy, 0.446 ct, the offshore network levy, 0.941 ct, and the § 19 StromNEV levy, 1.559 ct for the first GWh ([netztransparenz.de](https://www.netztransparenz.de/de-de/Erneuerbare-Energien-und-Umlagen)).

  In 2025 the levies were 0.277, 0.816 and 1.558 ct.
- **Supplier's margin:** 2.0 ct with VAT (1.68 ct without), about the middle of naturstrom smart's 1.19 ct, tado Hourly's 1.785 ct, Tibber's 2.15 ct and badenova Ökostrom Vario's 3.00 ct, all with VAT, with monthly fees of 4.58 to 8.50 EUR (October 2026).
- **The plan:** `de/westnetz-modul-3`: Westnetz is the largest grid operator, and Modul 3's low fee, from 00:00 to 07:00, is where an owner would set the car's schedule. Its prices match Westnetz's 2026 sheet: 0.95 ct low, 9.53 ct standard and 15.65 ct high, without VAT. Modul 3, due from grid operators since 1 April 2025 ([Bundesnetzagentur, 28 May 2026](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/DE/2026/20260528_14a.html)), is for a home with a controllable device over 4.2 kW, such as a wallbox put into operation since 1 January 2024 or an older one moved into the rules ([Bundesnetzagentur](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Vportal/Energie/SteuerbareVBE/start.html)), that chose it on top of Modul 1 and has an iMSys and no registered load measurement; it prices all grid power billed under that market location (Marktlokation), and the operator may bill the standard price in up to two quarters of the year ([Bundesnetzagentur, 17 July 2026](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Beschlusskammern/BK08/BK8_06_Netzentgelte/68_Para14a_EnWG/FAQ/start.html)).
- **Prices to download:** SMARD's series 4169, a week a file, like https://www.smard.de/app/chart_data/4169/DE/4169_DE_quarterhour_1766962800000.json. It matched Nord Pool's GER prices, which the board downloads, on all 4,992 quarter-hours from 10 August to 30 September 2026.
- **Uncertain:** the margins come from four offers only, and Tibber doesn't say whether its 2.15 ct includes VAT.
  - How many homes pay quarter-hour prices is not known. The Monitoringbericht 2026 is not out, its data having been collected from 16 March to 30 April 2026 ([Bundesnetzagentur](https://bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Fachthemen/ElektrizitaetundGas/Monitoringberichte/Datenerhebung/artikel.html)). A forsa survey in July 2024 had 7% of households say they use a dynamic tariff ([vzbv, October 2024](https://www.vzbv.de/sites/default/files/2024-10/Dynamische%20Stromtarife_repr%C3%A4sentative%20Befragung%202024.pdf)), more than the 1.1 million smart meters in homes and small businesses at the end of 2024 ([Bundesnetzagentur and Bundeskartellamt, table 48](https://data.bundesnetzagentur.de/Bundesnetzagentur/SharedDocs/Mediathek/Monitoringberichte/MonitoringberichtEnergie2025.pdf)), so it must include contracts billed without one.
  - The Bundesnetzagentur expects its 5.9% to be low, as not every competitive meter operator reports ([Bundesnetzagentur](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Fachthemen/ElektrizitaetundGas/NetzzugangMesswesen/Mess-undZaehlwesen/iMSys/artikel.html)), and says a fitted iMSys must also deliver quarter-hour values reliably ([Bundesnetzagentur, 21 September 2026](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/DE/2026/20260921_Rollout.html)); a supplier's switch to quarter-hour billing follows the fitting by several weeks to about three months ([Octopus](https://octopusenergy.de/dynamic-octopus), [Rabot](https://www.rabot.energy/fragen-antworten/), [naturstrom](https://www.naturstrom.de/privatkunden/oekostrom/naturstrom-smart)). Finanztip's 5.5% for the end of 2025 differs from the corrected 4.4% ([Finanztip](https://www.finanztip.de/stromtarife/dynamischer-stromtarif/)).
  - Whether tado bills quarter-hours is not stated on its pages.
  - Modul 3 reaches fewer homes still. A study based on two dynamic-tariff suppliers' experience found 14 of 169 grid operators, Westnetz among them, letting customers switch to it in May 2026 ([ZFK, 29 May 2026](https://www.zfk.de/energie/erneuerbare/zeitvariable-netzentgelte-umsetzung-netzbetreiber-modul-3)), and a Finanztip survey of 20 large suppliers found 7 letting customers use it, which EnBW disputes for itself ([pv magazine, 23 September 2026](https://www.pv-magazine.de/2026/09/23/viele-versorger-geben-netzentgelt-rabatte-nur-eingeschraenkt-an-die-kunden-weiter/)). The Bundesnetzagentur's list of enforcement showed only fine threats against two grid operators, with a deadline of 30 September 2026, when checked in October 2026 ([Bundesnetzagentur](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/1107472)), and it wants to open the modules further from 2027 if possible ([ZFK, 24 June 2026](https://www.zfk.de/politik/regulierung/zeitvariable-netzentgelte-bundesnetzagentur-haller-neuer-kurs)).
