# plans/lt/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges.

- **Dynamic contracts:** about 200,895 household contracts were dynamic or linked to the exchange at the end of 2025, 2.2% fewer than a year before ([VERT's 2025 report to the European Commission](https://www.ceer.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/NERC-Annual-Report-2025-EN.pdf)), which is 11.6% of the 1,727,209 household supply points of December 2025 ([VERT](https://www.vert.lt/elektra/SiteAssets/2026-02/Tiekimo%20rinka_apzvalga_sausis_2026.pdf)). In March 2026 Ignitis supplied 889,017 of the 1,731,071 supply points as an independent supplier and 441,016 (25.5%) as the public supplier, whose price VERT caps every half year ([VERT's report for the month](https://www.vert.lt/elektra/SiteAssets/2026-04/Tiekimo%20rinka_apzvalga_kovas_2026.pdf), [VERT on the cap](https://vert.lt/Puslapiai/naujienos/2025-metai/2025-05-21/nustatyta-UAB-Ignitis-visuomenines-elektros-kainos-virsutine-riba-2025-m-II-pusmeciui.aspx)). Ignitis said on 8 April 2025 that about 14.5% of its customers were on its monthly plan, which prices a month's average whatever the hour of use, about 3% on its hourly plan (now Išmanus 15) and more than 80% on fixed prices ([Ignitis](https://ignitis.lt/naujienos/ignitis-pataria-kaip-nesukti-galvos-del-elektros)); Enefit said on 19 August 2025 that about eight in ten of its customers were on fixed prices ([15min](https://www.15min.lt/verslas/naujiena/energetika/birzos-planas-ar-fiksuota-kaina-ekspertas-pasake-kuris-pasirinkimas-siandien-gali-buti-naudingesnis-664-2508162)) and Elektrum on 6 November 2025 that 7% of its customers, mostly businesses, were on exchange plans ([Elektrum](https://www.elektrum.lt/lt/namams/naujienos/naujienos/fiksuota-ar-birzos-kaina-ka-verta-zinoti-pries-pasirenkant)). Billing each quarter-hour of a home's own use takes a smart meter: Ignitis sells Išmanus 15 only to homes with one, weights each 15-minute price by the home's use in it and gives other homes its monthly plan ([Ignitis](https://ignitis.lt/lt/duk/nepriklausomas-elektros-tiekimas)), while Elektrum bills a home without one at the previous month's Nord Pool average weighted by ESO's 15-minute consumption coefficients ([Elektrum](https://www.elektrum.lt/lt/namams/duk/elektros-energija-privatiems-vartotojams/elektros-energijos-sutartis-privatiems-klientams/kaip-apskaiciuojama-kintamos-elektros-tarifo-kaina)). ESO had installed 1.3 million smart meters by 19 December 2025, starting with customers using more than 1,000 kWh a year, at about 66% of its customers and covering more than 90% of the country's consumption ([Ministry of Energy](https://enmin.lrv.lt/lt/naujienos/eso-baigia-pirmaji-ismaniuju-skaitikliu-diegimo-etapa-13-mln-skaitikliu-padengia-daugiau-nei-90-proc-salies-suvartojimo-rIZr/)).
- **VAT:** 21%.
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** -0.00026 EUR with VAT, unchanged from January to September 2026:
  - the public service obligations (VIAP), -0.00044 EUR without VAT, -0.00053 with it ([VERT O3E-1591](https://www.e-tar.lt/portal/lt/legalAct/1d881776b65c11f092fda1fd0c194cc5)), billed on top of ESO's fee;
  - the household extra component of the distribution service, 0.00022 EUR without VAT, 0.00027 with it (VERT O3E-1650, as [ESO](https://www.eso.lt/namams/elektra/tarifu-planai-kainos-atsiskaitymas/tarifu-planai-ir-kainos-2026-metais/4801) cites it);
  - no excise: households are exempt (Law on Excise Duties, art. 48(1)(3)).
- **Supplier's margin:** 0.025 EUR with VAT, about the middle of Elektrum Pažangus' 0.02456, Enefit Laisvas' 0.024 and Ignitis Išmanus 15's 0.02981, each with 2 EUR a month ([VERT's comparison](https://skaiciuokle.vert.lt/), updated 7 October 2026).
- **The plan:** `lt/eso-standartinis-4-zones`, whose night fee starts at 22:00. ESO's fee includes Litgrid's transmission, and its 2026 prices held from January to September (ESO's monthly price history). ESO's 2026 price rules (point 3.2) list the holidays with weekend zones, without Easter Monday or 2 November, which our four-zone plans have as holidays: to correct in plans/lt.
- **Prices to download:** Elering's dashboard, which republishes Nord Pool's Baltic prices, a month a file, like https://dashboard.elering.ee/api/nps/price?start=2026-01-01T00:00:00.000Z&end=2026-02-01T00:00:00.000Z (field data.lt). It matched Nord Pool's LT prices on all 4,992 quarter-hours from 10 August to 30 September 2026.
- **Uncertain:** the margins are September 2026's; earlier in 2026 they may have differed. No source counts the homes billed by the hour or quarter-hour: Ignitis' 3% is about 27,000 homes (8 April 2025, before its hourly plan became Išmanus 15 on 1 October 2025), and VERT's 200,895 less Ignitis' 14.5% on its monthly plan (about 129,000) leaves at most about 72,000 for all suppliers, 1.5% to 4% of the household supply points, with the dates mixed. VERT's 2025 count adds "exchange-linked plan contracts" to the "dynamic price contracts" of its 2024 report ([2024 report](https://www.ceer.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/NERC-Annual-Report-2024-EN.pdf)), so the 2.2% fall may not compare like with like. Enefit's page for its Laisvas plan says in English that the price changes every 15 minutes, and in Lithuanian that it changes every hour, noting Nord Pool's move to 15-minute intervals on 1 October 2025, and recommends a smart meter ([Enefit](https://www.enefit.lt/lt/privatiems/elektra/kintama-birzos-kaina)), but its terms for homes could not be read, so how it bills a home with or without one is unconfirmed. The share of households with a smart meter is not published for 2026: ESO's 66% counts all its customers, while VERT gave 53% of households in early 2025 ([2024 report](https://www.ceer.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/NERC-Annual-Report-2024-EN.pdf)) and Elektrum more than 60% in November 2025 ([Elektrum](https://www.elektrum.lt/lt/namams/naujienos/naujienos/fiksuota-ar-birzos-kaina-ka-verta-zinoti-pries-pasirenkant)).
