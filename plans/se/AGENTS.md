# plans/se/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges.

- **Dynamic contracts:** a minority, about one in seven. SCB counted 14.1% of retail contracts as quarter-hour or hourly priced in August 2026 (14.4% in SE3), against 61.0% on a monthly price and 7.5% on a fixed price of one to three years ([SCB](https://www.statistikdatabasen.scb.se/pxweb/sv/ssd/START__EN__EN0301__EN0301A/SSDManadElAvtalstyp/)); the share stayed between 12.5% and 15.1% in the twelve months to August 2026, and Ei gave 13.6% for December 2024 ([Ei](https://ei.se/download/18.3f4d315019a0f5489b07296/1761916461843/Sveriges-el-och-naturgasmarknad-2024-Ei-R2025-12.pdf)). A monthly price is set after the month, at Vattenfall from the spot prices and the use of all its monthly-metered customers in the area, so a home pays for the group's pattern, not its own ([Vattenfall](https://www.vattenfall.se/fokus/tips-rad/skillnad-kvartspris-rorligt-elpris/)). Since Nord Pool went to quarter-hours on 1 October 2025, every supplier with over 200,000 customers must offer a quarter-hour contract ([Ei](https://ei.se/konsument/el/elmatare-och-matning-av-din-elanvandning/fragor-och-svar-om-kvartsmatning)), and Elpriskollen listed 132 quarter-hour and 5 hourly contracts for an SE3 postcode on 11 October 2026. Nearly all of Sweden's 5.7 million metering points were equipped for quarter-hours in May 2025 ([Ei](https://ei.se/om-oss/nyheter/2025/2025-05-21-ei-har-granskat-elnatsforetagens-formaga-att-mata-och-rapportera-kvartsmatvarden)), but by default a home with a main fuse up to 63 A is metered and reported once a month, unless its contract needs quarter-hours or it asks ([Förordning 1999:716](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/forordning-1999716-om-matning-berakning-och_sfs-1999-716/), 6 b and 6 c §§). Vattenfall asks the grid company to change the meter at no cost and bills the monthly price until it has ([Vattenfall](https://www.vattenfall.se/kundservice/fragor-och-svar/elavtal-och-elpriser/)); the grid company must report the change to the supplier within 40 days of the supplier's request ([Ei](https://ei.se/bransch/matning-av-el/fragor-och-svar-om-elmatning)).
- **VAT:** 25%.
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** the energy tax, 0.360 SEK without VAT, 0.450 with it, in 2026 ([Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/skatterochavdrag/punktskatter/energiskatter/skattpael), SFS 2025:1357), billed by the grid company; 0.439 and 0.54875 in 2025, and 0.096 less in some northern municipalities. Electricity certificates cost about 0.0002 SEK, inside suppliers' prices; the authority fees are yearly.
- **Supplier's margin:** 0.12 SEK with VAT (0.096 without), with the variable costs suppliers add, about the middle of the five largest: Tibber 0.119, Vattenfall 0.134, E.ON 0.099, Fortum 0.112 and Göteborg Energi 0.135 SEK with VAT for September 2026, with monthly fees of 45 to 69 SEK ([Ei's Elpriskollen](https://elpriskollen.se/)).
- **The plan:** `se/vattenfall-tidstariff`: Vattenfall Eldistribution is the largest grid company, and its Tidstariff costs more on weekdays from 06:00 to 22:00, November to March, so its low fee starts at 22:00. Its prices match the [2026 price list](https://www.vattenfalleldistribution.se/globalassets/1.-privat/abonnemang-och-avgifter/om-avtal-och-avgifter/elnatsavgifter/sakringsabonnemang/prislista-sakringsabonnemang-privat-2026-01-01.pdf): 0.305 and 0.765 SEK with VAT. In 2025 they were 0.268 and 0.670, and weekday holidays counted as high load.
- **State support:** households in SE3 got back 0.26 SEK per kWh used in January and February 2026 (SFS 2026:502), paid apart from the bill, so it doesn't change which hours are cheapest.
- **Prices to download:** elprisetjustnu.se, a day a file, like https://www.elprisetjustnu.se/api/v1/prices/2026/01-01_SE3.json. Its EUR_per_kWh matched Nord Pool's SE3 prices in euros on all 4,992 quarter-hours from 10 August to 30 September 2026; its own kronor come at another rate, so the program converts the euros at the ECB's daily rate, within half a percent of Nord Pool's kronor.
- **Uncertain:** the margins' variable costs change monthly. SCB does not say whether its contract counts cover homes only: its monthly survey asks suppliers for contracts by type and area, with no split by customer, and it warns that a wrong contract type in a supplier's report is hard to spot ([SCB](https://www.scb.se/contentassets/1e73cca715aa4bfba4f951d86537bf59/en0301/en0301_kd_2026_v1_20260522.pdf)), while Ei's report calls the same figures household customers' ([Ei](https://ei.se/download/18.3f4d315019a0f5489b07296/1761916461843/Sveriges-el-och-naturgasmarknad-2024-Ei-R2025-12.pdf)). No figure was found for the share among electric car owners or for the homes still waiting for a meter change: in May 2025 Ei counted 15,849 low-voltage metering points without a smart meter and 13 grid companies not yet reporting quarter-hour values for points settled on those values, which they promised to fix by 11 June 2025, with no later count ([Ei](https://ei.se/om-oss/nyheter/2025/2025-05-21-ei-har-granskat-elnatsforetagens-formaga-att-mata-och-rapportera-kvartsmatvarden)).
