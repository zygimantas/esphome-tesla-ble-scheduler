# plans/lv/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges. Prices are in euros per kWh.

- **Dynamic contracts:** available, and about one home in six has one. At the end of June 2026, 15.73% of homes (143,372) had an exchange-price (biržas cena) contract, 75.69% a fixed price and 8.58% the universal service, also fixed: SPRK's review of the second quarter, 8 September 2026, https://www.sprk.gov.lv/lv/jaunums/dubultojies-latvijas-eksportetais-elektroenergijas-daudzums-elektroenergijas-tirgus-tendencu-2026-gada-2-ceturksna-apskats (16.02% at the end of 2025, in its review of 25 February 2026). A supplier with over 200,000 customers must offer one (Electricity Market Law, art. 32.3). They bill Nord Pool's day-ahead price for the LV area in each quarter-hour, weighted by the home's metered use in it, plus the supplier's fee: Elektrum, Enefit, Tet, Ignitis and Virši all say the price changes every 15 minutes, as does Sadales tīkls' tariff page. But from 1 October 2025 to 31 January 2026 Sadales tīkls split each hour's metered kWh into four equal quarter-hours, and that data stays: https://sadalestikls.lv/lv/preses-relizes/no-februara-sadales-tikls-klientiem-nodrosinas-faktiskos-15-minusu-uzskaites-datus (27 January 2026). So those four months were billed at each hour's average of its four prices. Real quarter-hour data started on 1 February 2026, first billed in March. On those 122 nights, the cheapest quarter-hours of each night cost 12% more at their hours' averages, about 8 euros with VAT in all, while charging at once barely changes.
- **VAT:** 21%, the standard rate, on every part of the bill: Pievienotās vērtības nodokļa likums, art. 41, https://likumi.lv/ta/id/253451-pievienotas-vertibas-nodokla-likums. Its 12% rate for homes covers heat, not electricity (art. 42), and the suppliers' pages say "ar PVN 21%". The same in October to December 2025.
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** nothing besides the margin, 0.00000 with VAT.
  - The mandatory procurement component (OIK): 0.00000 without and with VAT. It has been 0 since 1 September 2022, and since 1 May 2023 the state budget covers its costs instead of the bills: Electricity Market Law, art. 33(6), https://likumi.lv/ta/id/108834-elektroenergijas-tirgus-likums, and the public trader, https://www.eptirgotajs.lv/vairak-par-oi/.
  - The electricity tax, 0.00101 without VAT: 0.00000, as homes are exempt: Elektroenerģijas nodokļa likums, art. 6(2)(4), https://likumi.lv/ta/id/150692-elektroenergijas-nodokla-likums.
  - The transmission grid's fee is inside Sadales tīkls' fee, so in the plan.
  - The balancing capacity fee (balansēšanas jaudas uzturēšanas maksa) of AST, the transmission operator, set for each month: in 2026 0.00122 - 0.00387 without VAT, 0.00252 on average from January to September, 0.00305 with VAT; in October to December 2025 0.00300, 0.00363 with VAT: https://www.ast.lv/lv/content/regulesanas-sistemas-nebalansa-un-balansesanas-jaudas-uzturesanas-cenas. Elektrum includes it in its fee, while Enefit, Virši and Ignitis add it as a line of its own, so the margin below includes it. Listed apart, it's 0.00305 with VAT, and the margin without it about 0.01100.
  - Monthly, left out: Sadales tīkls' capacity fee by the main fuse (Pamata 0.34 euros per ampere on one phase, 0.82 on three, without VAT), which state support cut for homes of 16 - 25 A until 31 December 2025; the suppliers' monthly fees; the protected users' discount of 20 - 25 euros. No price cap or support per kWh was found for 2025 or 2026.
- **Supplier's margin:** 0.01400 with VAT (0.01157 without), with AST's balancing fee in it. Most homes buy from Elektrum (SPRK's concentration index for homes, 0.52, means one supplier has more than half), whose fee is 0.01498; the others come to 0.00944 - 0.01430 with the balancing fee. The offers on 11 October 2026, with VAT (without VAT in brackets):
  - Elektrum Dinamiskais: 0.01498 (0.01238) from 50 kWh a month, balancing included, and 1.00 - 2.50 euros a month, 1.00 above 400 kWh: https://www.elektrum.lv/lv/majai/klientiem/elektribas-podukta-izvele/elektroenergijas-produkti/.
  - Enefit Dinamiskais: 0.01100 (0.00909), and the balancing fee on the bill, 0.01405 (0.01161) in all; its page shows no monthly fee: https://www.enefit.lv/lv/majai/elektriba.
  - Tet Dinamiskais+: 0.00944 (0.00780) and 2.00 euros a month; the older Tet Dinamiskais 0.01430 (0.01182) and none. Tet lists no balancing fee apart: price list from 10 August 2026, https://www.tet.lv/par-mums/cenradis.
  - Ignitis Aktīvais: 0.00970 (0.00802) and 2.00 euros a month, until 3 November 2026, and the balancing fee, 0.01275 (0.01053) in all: https://ignitis.lv/elektriba-majsaimniecibam.
  - Virši Dinamiskā elektrība: 0.00920 (0.00760) and 1.98 euros a month, and the balancing fee on the bill, 0.01225 (0.01012) in all: https://www.virsi.lv/lv/privatpersonam/elektriba/elektriba.
  - energija24.lv's comparison of 5 October 2026 has the same fees. Ignitis also sells Naksnīgais to EV owners: a price fixed for 12 months, 0.07600 from 00:00 to 06:00 every night and 0.16110 at other hours, and 2.00 euros a month. For the car alone, that's about what the cheapest quarter-hours of each night cost this year on an exchange contract with this margin, 0.07585.
- **The plan:** lv/sadales-tikls-pamata. About 97% of homes are on Pamata (Sadales tīkls, 5 December 2025), and every new connection starts on it. Its fee is the same in every hour of every day, with no holiday rules, so no cheap fee starts at night: 0.03962 without VAT, 0.04794 with VAT, since 1 January 2024 and all of 2026, at https://sadalestikls.lv/lv/tarifi. That matches the plan, and Speciālais' 0.15848 (0.19176) matches its own; neither changed during 2026. On 1 January 2027 Pamata drops to 0.03865 (0.04677), as the plan's comment says (Latvijas Vēstnesis 2026/145, which SPRK accepted on 19 August 2026), and Jaudīgais starts: 0.02010 (0.02432) and 1.40 euros per ampere a month on three phases instead of 0.82, so cheaper than Pamata above about 625 - 1,000 kWh a month on 20 - 32 A, which a home that charges a car may reach. sadalestikls.lv answered WebFetch with HTTP 451; curl with a browser's user agent got the page. enefit.lv and ast.lv refuse curl and open in a browser.
- **Prices to download:** Elering's dashboard API, which republishes Nord Pool's prices without an account, a month a file: https://dashboard.elering.ee/api/nps/price?start=2026-01-01T00:00:00.000Z&end=2026-02-01T00:00:00.000Z, field data.lv, in euros per MWh, each entry at its quarter-hour's start in Unix seconds, the end's included. October 2025 to September 2026 is complete, 96 quarter-hours a day. It's the price the exchange contracts bill: it matched Nord Pool's own LV prices on 15 and 19 September 2026 in all 96 quarter-hours. Read data.lv, not data.lt: the two areas split at times, as in 517 of March 2026's 2,977 quarter-hours and 27 on 19 September.
- **Uncertain:** the suppliers' fees in October to December 2025, as only today's offers were found; Enefit's monthly fee; whether Tet's fees include AST's balancing fee, as energija24.lv says of its 0.01430, and whether Ignitis bills it apart, as energija24.lv's 0.00363 for it suggests; how the exchange contracts split between suppliers, as SPRK gives only the total; whether every supplier bills a negative price as negative. With one grid fee at all hours, the car's own schedule has no natural start: the night's prices fall from midnight, and on the market prices the cheapest fixed start over the year was 04:30.
