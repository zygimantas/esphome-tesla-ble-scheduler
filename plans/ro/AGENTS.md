# plans/ro/

Research for the README's Example of yearly savings (test/savings_example.cpp), checked in October 2026: what a kWh costs here besides the market price and the plan's fee. Monthly fees are left out, as they don't depend on when the car charges.

- **Dynamic contracts:** offered, but no household has one. The energy law (Legea 123/2012, art. 62 (1) i^1) gives anyone with a working smart meter the right to one from any supplier with over 200,000 customers: in 2025 Electrica Furnizare, E.ON Energie România, PPC Energie, Premier Energy and Hidroelectrica. ANRE's report on 2025 (July 2026, https://anre.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Raport-2025-monitorizare-CPD.pdf) counted 288 customers on dynamic contracts, all businesses, and no household. The meter must be in the operator's smart metering system (SMI), which reached 34.5% of low-voltage users at the end of 2025 and about 14% in DEER's area (https://anre.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/RAPORT_implem_SMI_cf_CISMI_2019-2028_rezultate_la_31_12_2025.pdf). The offers bill OPCOM's day-ahead price (PZU) in lei for each 15-minute interval the meter records, plus a supply component and the charges below. The grid injection fee (TG) counts as part of that price and isn't added. They are ENGIE's Elec Flexibil, Electrica Dinamic, Hidroelectrica's Hidro Dinamic C, Premier Energy's PE Dinamic and, for prosumers only, PPC MaxControl, which splits an hourly reading evenly over its quarter-hours. Households almost all pay one price for every hour: in June 2026, 73% of the 8.82 million household customers had a competitive offer and 27% universal service (ANRE's monthly report, https://anre.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Monit-iun26.pdf). The price cap, 0.68 - 1.30 lei/kWh with VAT, ended on 30 June 2025 (OUG 6/2025, https://www.ppcenergy.ro/energie-electrica/plafonare/). One supplier sells cheap hours instead: PPC's Ore Smart+ (smart meter, no prosumers) charges 0.36100 lei/kWh before network fees, taxes and VAT at weekends and on weekdays 00:00 - 06:00, also 11:00 - 15:00 from March to October, and 0.85700 at other times (https://www.ppcenergy.ro/wp-content/uploads/oferta-ppc-ore-smart.pdf, October 2026).
- **VAT:** 21% since 1 August 2025, up from 19% (Legea 141/2025, changing art. 291 of the Fiscal Code: https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/L_141_2025.pdf). It stayed 21% from October 2025 to September 2026 and still is in October 2026. Electricity has no reduced rate.
- **Per kWh, the same every hour:** 2026's, in lei per kWh without and with VAT. Transelectrica's fee for taking power from the grid (TL), 0.03645 and 0.04410 (ANRE Order 74/2025, https://anre.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Ord-74-2025.pdf). System services, 0.01470 and 0.01779 (Order 73/2025, https://anre.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Ord-73-2025.pdf). Green certificates, 0.07402 and 0.08956: ANRE's quota of 0.499387 certificates per MWh (Order 81/2025, https://anre.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Ord-81-2025.pdf) times the previous month's certificate price, as ENGIE and Electrica billed them all year. The high-efficiency cogeneration contribution, 0.01450 and 0.01755 from 1 July 2026 (Order 49/2026, https://anre.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Ord-49-2026.pdf), 0.01360 and 0.01646 from 1 November 2025 (Order 66/2025). Excise for non-business use, 0.00768 and 0.00929: the Fiscal Code's 4.74 lei/MWh (annex 1 of title VIII) raised with inflation every January (art. 342), as Digi Energy lists it (https://www.digi.ro/servicii/digi-energy/tarife). The contribution for contracts for difference (CfD), 0.00014 and 0.00017 (0.000144 exactly, Order 69/2025, https://anre.ro/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Ord-69-2025.pdf). Their sum is 0.14749 without VAT and 0.17847 with VAT, or 0.17738 with the cogeneration contribution of January - June 2026. Checked against Electrica's list of regulated fees (https://www.electricafurnizare.ro/asistenta/tarife-reglementate-transport-distributie-energie-electrica-contributie-cogenerare-energie-reactiva/), Transelectrica's report of 19 December 2025 and ENGIE's monthly offers. The grid charges homes no monthly or per-kW fee; some fixed offers add a daily fee, and the dynamic ones found don't. October - December 2025 cost less: TL 0.03303, system services 0.01279, green certificates 0.07254, cogeneration 0.00840 in October and 0.01360 from November, excise 0.00724 and CfD 0.00021, in all 0.16239 with VAT in October and 0.16868 in November and December. From 1 October 2026, after the year studied, system services drop to 0.01272 (Digi's page, PPC's October offers).
- **Supplier's margin:** 0.24200 lei/kWh with VAT. It's ENGIE's supply component (CF), 0.20000 without VAT on top of the market price, the same in its Elec Flexibil offers from January to September 2026, with no monthly fee (the offer of 23 September 2026: https://www.engie.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Oferta-Elec-Flexibil-B2C.pdf). The others: Premier Energy's PE Dinamic of December 2025, 0.19000 plus an estimated 0.15000 for imbalances, 0.41140 with VAT, no fee, and the customer forecasts each quarter-hour (https://www.premierenergy.info/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Premier-Oferta-Dinamic-_casnic-OEDC_12_2025.pdf); PPC MaxControl of October 2026, for prosumers only, 0.09500, 0.11495 with VAT, with the market price held between 0 and 0.999 lei/kWh (https://www.ppcenergy.ro/wp-content/uploads/oferta-ppc-maxcontrol-rezidential.pdf); Hidroelectrica's Hidro Dinamic C at its launch in November 2023, 0.07300 plus 0.14000 for imbalances, as a news site reported (https://e-nergia.ro/hidroelectrica-a-lansat-oferta-de-energie-electrica-pentru-populatie-cu-pret-dinamic-care-se-poate-schimba-de-la-o-ora-la-alta).
- **The plan:** ro/deer-jt. DEER serves the most homes: about 4.0 of Romania's 10.4 million low-voltage users (39%), against 3.2 million for Rețele Electrice, 1.6 for Delgaz Grid and 1.5 for Oltenia (worked out from the smart metering report above). Its fee is the same in every hour, as Romania has no time-of-use network fees, so no cheap fee starts at night. Its 0.42996 lei/kWh with VAT, the sum of 31.96, 83.36 and 240.02 lei/MWh (ANRE Order 77/2025, https://anre.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Ord-77-2025.pdf), matches DEER's page and 2026's prices, unchanged through October 2026 (ENGIE's September and PPC's October offers still use 0.35534 without VAT). In 2025 it was 0.42463 with VAT (350.93 lei/MWh). A car's own schedule has no fee to follow: on OPCOM's prices, which fall after 21:00, the cheapest night hour began around 04:15 on average (every fifth day from October 2025 to September 2026).
- **Prices to download:** OPCOM's day-ahead results, a CSV for each delivery day, with no account: https://www.opcom.ro/rapoarte-pzu-raportPIP-export-csv/DD/MM/YYYY/ro?resolution=15, for example https://www.opcom.ro/rapoarte-pzu-raportPIP-export-csv/01/10/2025/ro?resolution=15. Rows "Romania","n","price" give lei per MWh for quarter-hour n, counted from 00:00 CET, which is 01:00 in Romania; clock-change days have 92 or 100. The answer's type is text/html, and later files add a Rezolutie column. It's the price the dynamic contracts bill: ENGIE's list of August 2026 prices repeats OPCOM's, and Nord Pool's TEL prices in RON, which the board downloads, matched OPCOM's within 0.03 lei/MWh on 15 September 2026. Energy-Charts has the same prices in euros (https://api.energy-charts.info/price?bzn=RO&start=2025-10-01T00:00%2B02:00&end=2025-10-01T23:45%2B02:00), which OPCOM converts at one rate a day (5.0783 for 1 October 2025, as the ECB's of 29 September), but it answered repeated requests with HTTP 429 and allows private use only.
- **Uncertain:** E.ON's and Hidroelectrica's sites refused automated requests, and Electrica publishes its dynamic contract with the supply component left blank, so their current margins are unknown; ENGIE's July and August offers don't print the 0.20000. The green certificate charge follows the certificate price month by month and is settled again the next September; suppliers billed 0.07402 all through 2026. The system services fee of 1 October 2026 isn't on ANRE's list of orders yet. The cheapest night hour comes from 73 sample days.
