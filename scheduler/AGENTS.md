# scheduler/

- ESPHome compiles every C and C++ file in this folder into the firmware, so tests, tools and scratch code live elsewhere. Other files, like this one, are fine.
- charger.h, settings.h and the headers they include, calendar.h, market.h, savings.h, schedule.h and tariff.h, are built as plain C++17 by the unit tests, without ESPHome, and as gnu++20 in the firmware. Keep them C++17 and free of ESPHome headers. test/mutation_test.py lists the seven: a new header goes there too.
- The settings are a file on the board, which the page's form writes or the user uploads, and which read_settings() in settings.h reads and checks before the board saves it, and again at every start. A board's first settings apply at once, as nothing runs yet, and device.yaml's Bluetooth lambda hands the new VIN to the Tesla part; later ones restart the board. The page's setup saves its first step's as settings without market: and tariff:, which read_settings() takes as no prices yet: the car charges as usual until the setup's last step adds them. A new setting needs its place in read_settings()'s table, its check, its row in README.md's Settings, and its field in web/web_ui.js if the form shows it. A check made stricter turns away settings a board saved before: the page then asks for them again.
- Entity names are an interface. The page finds the board's entities by name (web/web_ui.js), this component finds the Tesla's by name, and Ready by and Ready by once are saved under keys ESPHome makes from their names. Renaming an entity, or an ESPHome release that makes those keys differently, silently loses the saved values: check after upgrading ESPHome.
- esphome-tesla-ble, at the pinned `tesla_ble_ref`: the "Charger" binary sensor keeps its state once the car has reported it (only the asleep and user-present sensors go back to unknown); with the charging state "Unknown" it also reports the Charger as off, which isn't an unplug; the battery level and charge limit keep their last values; the charging states are "Disconnected", "No Power", "Starting", "Charging", "Complete", "Stopped", "Calibrating" and "Unknown". The controller relies on all of these.
- Savings goes to flash byte for byte under the key "scheduler_savings", and ESPHome loads it only into a struct of the same size, which a static_assert holds: another size starts every board's figures afresh, and another layout of the same size misreads them. To change it, read the old layout under its key and save the new one under another.
- Controller::observe_() runs before the schedule is made, so it judges a start from the car against the previous tick's schedule. On the first tick after a restart there's none, and in_schedule_() is true.
